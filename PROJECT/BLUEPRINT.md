# BLUEPRINT — *A Nascent Light* (Isekai / LitRPG web serial)

> **Tone contract:** mature, grounded, pragmatic. Characters react logically to progression.
> No forced edginess, no grimdark, no anime melodrama. Humor is dry, self-aware (per donor voice:
> *I just want to learn magic bro*). Progression must feel *earned*: understanding unlocks power, never entitlement.
> **Remix rule:** prose is lifted from the Resource Bank and edited; original sentences only where the bank is silent.

---

## 1. Premise
Daniel Reyes, 28, IT support tech, died of a heart attack in his sleep. In the white room between, a
nameless **Attendant** offers him a job as host of a devolving relic-system — the **Nascent Kaleidoscope** —
a shattered "prism of worlds" that needs a stubborn, *ordinary* soul to reassemble it one facet at a time.
He wakes eleven years into the life of **Kale Vester**, a frontier trapper's son in the March of
**Eastermark**, where his late-blooming mind finally surfaces ("foreign thoughts… too vivid to be daydreams" —
ANK Ch1 structure). The system finishes booting around him, and the story begins proper: a slow, complete
life of hunting, learning, contracts, and light — not a race to godhood.

## 2. Protagonist — Kale Vester (b. "Daniel Reyes")
- 11 at story start. Lean, weather-toughened from years of chores; red-brown hair (father's), grey eyes.
- Adult patience + child's body = competence fetish (cooking, knotwork, planning) — donor: user's *magic bro* opening.
- **Flaw:** emotional dampening from the system's calm makes him *flat*, occasionally inhuman; the serial's
  interior arc is re-learning to feel at full volume. His family is the anchor he refuses to lose.
- Motive (borrowed & re-cut from donor motives): not revenge, not a demon king — *security, then mastery,
  then the right to keep the people he feeds.* "I just want to learn how the world works, properly." (Heliel43 template)
  and, inherited from ANK's MC: knowledge as domain, secrets as leverage, home as choice.

## 3. The System — Nascent Kaleidoscope ("Prism")
UI in `[brackets]`, dry procedural voice (Tensura/GGJ donor style). Nothing is free.

- **Stats:** HP, SP, **LUM** (lumens = mana pool), then VIT/STR/DEX/END/INT/WIS/MAG/LUK (GGJ chassis).
  Racial modifiers exist (Human: jack-of-all-trades blurb — lifted from GGJ race picker).
- **Facets** (passives): Prism Calm (Gamer's Mind remix: fear/emotion damp; hidden cost), Regeneration,
  Adaptation, later Adept-learner (GGJ trait, re-flavored as "Fast Refraction").
- **Refractions** (actives, leveled x/10): Mirror (Observe+appraise; *levels only when he genuinely
  understands the target*), Frost Lance/Frost Hand (Draugr's chanted frost), Light-Snap (flash), Lumen Step (dash),
  Skill upgrades spend **Motes** (harvest of dying will — from Draugr's "A Soul has been absorbed" — here:
  taken from a *just-dead* creature he himself killed or euthanized; moral texture, not edge).
- **Classes/Titles:** Title "Nascent" at boot; class slot *sealed* until Arc 3 (pragmatic pacing — no instant-build god).
- **Quests:** the Attendant installs tutorial-style quests with *sensible* rewards; later quests self-generate
  from goals the system deems "core-refractive." Konosuba gag allowed once ("[Status]… that usually works in those light novels").
- **The Shard:** reliquary relic from Greyfell Mound (Ch6) — a second prism splinter fused to him; unlocks
  **Refraction Slots +1** and the end-of-arc milestone class seed. Foreshadow: the Kaleidoscope was *deliberately broken*
  into splinters scattered across worlds; each recovered splinter makes him a target (Arc 3+ antagonist: the **Order of the Closed Eye**, splinter-hunters).
- Power curve discipline: ~1 level/2–4 chapters early; every tier jump costs a real decision, injury, or insight.

## 4. Setting — Eastermark (original, assembled from donor textures)
Frontier marches beyond the last charted keep. **Garrow's Ford**: 200 souls, palisade, sheep, pine and birch,
the **Greywood**, the river **Fordwater**, ruined **Windhollow Keep**, and the sealed **Greyfell Mound** (a
"howe" of the old kings — barrow culture lifted from Hobbit-barrow-downs register + Draugr mechanics).
Winter is a character. **Halloway** (baronial seat, ~4 days south) → **Saltmarsh** (walled town, Delvers'
Assembly hall) for Arc 2+. Currency: silver *marks*, copper *pence*; a laborer eats on ~20d/day (konosuba price realism).
No "adventurer" prestige — **Delvers** are licensed grave-schappers and ruin-clearers, legally half-criminal (donor: Dost Thou Even Steal's policing texture).

- **Signe Vester** (mother, late 30s): village midwife & hedger; first to notice "the boy watched her *differently* after that winter fever." (donor: Borne-of-Caution caregiver + Art-of-Burning found-family)
- **Astrid** (sister, 8→9): feral joy, Kale's soft spot; collects teeth.
- **Old Runa** (grandmother): storehouse of legend; gives the mound-warnings folkline (Hobbit register).
- **Bailiff Soren Kel** (Halloway's taxman): law as a ledger; not evil, merely baron's.
- **Delvers' crew (Tresh crew):** **Benn Tresh** (quartermaster-negotiator; Breaking-the-World haggling voice; his brother **Halvard** died in the howe), **Cato** (aged hedge-mage; ex-apprentice-filer for the Order of the Closed Eye; mentor-technician), **Brant** (shield-man, deadpan teacher — *Ove is the village smith's boy; don't conflate*), **Maren Skell** (16, outrider — see below).
- **Maren Skell** — *the slow burn.* Foundling ex-slave-trainee, taciturn, professionally lethal, terrible at being
  kind but excellent at *showing* it (Din Djarin template from The Stowaway, fully gendered & original).
  Her motive (donor-pasted from The Stowaway's reader-arc): earn a hearth of her own by never being caught;
  ends the debt that owns her crew-share. Romance pacing: Ch7 respect → Vol.2 mentorship/trust → Vol.3–4
  wounds & shared-bed beats → Vol.5+ the burn catches. Zero smut until both are adults and it's *narratively due*.
- Antagonists (arc-late, seeded now): the **Closed Eye** prospectors (a dead crewman's journal in Ch7),
  **Stefan Drask** (Halloway's second son, splinter-collector patron), and what actually opened Greyfell from the inside.

## 6. Volume / Arc map
- **Vol. 1 "First Light"** (Ch1–8, Kale 11): white room → system boot → first kill → Greyfell Mound → the crew.
  Ends: Kale bound to the Tresh crew as paid runner/apprentice with the sealed contract & the shard in his chest.
- **Vol. 2 "The Assembly"** (Ch9–20, 12–13): Saltmarsh registration, E-rank crawl, winter quarter, first real party
  dynamics, Class unlock (**Kaleidoscope Adept — Mirrorwright build**), Drask notice, Maren's debt revealed.
- **Vol. 3 "Long Waters"** (Ch21–34): river expedition to a drowned barrow; Closed Eye first contact;
  splinter #2; a death in the crew (Ove's brother? or Benn's ruin) → consequences; Mirror 20s.
- **Vol. 4 "Hearth & Ledger"** (Ch34–48): politics of Halloway barony, harvest & homestead upgrade, bond
  with Maren turns (first kiss at equal adulthood-delayed to epilogue-ish beats), splinter #3 in a fae-glade.
- **Vol. 5 "The Eye Opens"** (Ch49–64): anti-quest vs. Drask & the Closed Eye; the Attendant's silence explained;
  the Kaleidoscope's makers; Eastermark at war. (Volumes past 2 are *roadmap, not leash* — follow material.)
- Interludes allowed (crew POV, Runa's folktales) when donor prose supports them; never as filler.

## 7. Style bible (editing rules for every chapter)
1. First-person past tense, adult-voice, dry humor; short self-aware beats, no capering.
2. System boxes: bracketed, clipped, procedural. Emotion between boxes, not inside them.
3. No exclamation-mark hype; numbers appear only when they *mean* something on-page.
4. Every lifted line gets checked for: name-continuity, tense, IP residue, and stat-consistency with STATE.md ledger.
5. Scenes end on a beat: progression milestone, cost paid, or quiet dread — never a fake cliffhanger scream.
6. Downtime is content (chores, cooking, mending, meals) — donor: Art of Burning / Borne of Caution.
