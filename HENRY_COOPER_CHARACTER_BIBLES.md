# Henry Cooper — Character Bibles

**Status:** pre-chapter planning; no character scene is final prose
**Purpose:** keep MCU characters recognizable, keep original characters useful rather than decorative, and separate author knowledge from viewpoint knowledge.

Every core character has:

- a surface want;
- a deeper need;
- a fear or protective lie;
- a contradiction;
- a voice and physical habit;
- a relationship function;
- a knowledge boundary;
- a failure mode;
- a growth direction.

A character bible is not permission to make every character explain their psychology aloud. Most of these facts should be visible through choices.

---

## 1. Henry Cooper

**Role:** protagonist; novice masked hero; hidden Jotun/Asgardian child
**Opening age:** approximately 13–14 in fall 2014
**Apparent birth:** 2000 foundling record
**Actual birth:** 1972, after conception during the 1971 D.B. Cooper period
**Home:** Queens, under the guardianship of Elena Reyes
**School:** Midtown School of Science and Technology, same cohort/class as Peter
**POV:** primary first-person narrator

### Surface want

Henry wants to keep ordinary control over his life: attend school, avoid being moved again, keep Elena from worrying, and understand his powers well enough not to hurt someone.

### Deeper need

He needs to accept that being cared for is not the same as being owned, and that usefulness is not the price of belonging.

### Protective lie

> If I stay useful, prepared, and difficult to abandon, nobody can decide I am disposable.

### Fear

Being moved, classified, or claimed by people who know facts about him that he does not. He fears losing the few ordinary relationships he has more than he fears physical danger.

### Contradictions

- He wants a normal life and keeps volunteering for abnormal danger.
- He distrusts secrets but maintains several himself.
- He is cautious about power but impulsive about people in immediate danger.
- He dislikes grand speeches but has a private hunger for a family explanation.
- He can take care of others before he can admit he needs care.

### Voice

Practical, quick, occasionally dry. He notices the cost of things: water bottles, damaged shoes, bus schedules, ruined homework, how much food he needs after a large freeze. He jokes most when a direct emotional answer would expose him. He is not a tiny Tony, Loki, or Peter.

### Physical habits

- checks the nearest water source and exits without meaning to;
- rubs his fingers when numbness begins;
- hides a headache by becoming more sarcastic;
- keeps one hand near a railing or pipe in unfamiliar buildings;
- checks whether people are watching before he uses even a small amount of power;
- later learns to carry electrolyte packets and spare gloves.

### Power relationship

Henry initially thinks of the power as a dangerous body problem. He progresses through controlled experiments, movement, rescue, and judgment. He is not interested in “attacks” as a category until he needs one. Ice is first a way to hold, brace, redirect, slow, cool, and escape.

### Relationship functions

- **Peter:** peer, teacher in rescue, friend, secret-identity joke, chosen brother without automatic family language.
- **Elena:** home, ordinary accountability, practical care, the person who knew Henry before the mask.
- **Thor:** limited acquaintance before reveal; post-reveal family possibility earned by behavior.
- **Loki:** biological father who has no right to immediate access and no skill at ordinary fatherhood.
- **Sylvie:** person who saved him and also made an enormous decision without his consent.
- **TVA:** the institution that turned his life into a case number.

### Knowledge boundary at opening

Henry knows he is an orphan/foundling under a long-term guardian, has an absent biological father, and has begun manipulating ice. He does not know Loki, D.B. Cooper, the actual 1972 birth, the TVA, Sylvie, or why his records are approximate.

### Failure modes

- hides a serious injury;
- freezes a problem instead of communicating;
- assumes adults will either use him or abandon him;
- confuses a boundary with rejection;
- treats his own body as expendable when someone else is in danger;
- uses humor to make a real conversation impossible.

### Growth markers

1. tells Peter the truth about an injury;
2. lets Elena see the mask before she discovers it accidentally;
3. refuses a dangerous Avengers role without feeling cowardly;
4. asks Thor a question rather than waiting to be protected;
5. tells Loki “you cannot decide this for me” and stays in the conversation;
6. accepts help in the final TVA crisis without surrendering the decision;
7. keeps or changes the mask because he chooses it.

### Prohibited drift

Henry does not become omniscient, emotionally invulnerable, cruel for edge, or uniquely important to every canon plot. He can be legitimate without being the strongest person in the room.

## 2. Peter Parker / Spider-Man

**Role:** Henry's first masked mentor/peer and closest friend
**Opening age:** approximately 13–14 under the working MCU school placement
**Status at opening:** early Spider-Man with unseen patrol experience
**Canon protection:** remains the Peter recruited by Tony in *Civil War* and the central character of the Spider-Man films

### Surface want

Peter wants to help people, keep his identity safe, remain useful to the adults he admires, and still have a normal school life.

### Deeper need

He needs to understand that responsibility includes letting trusted people share danger and truth, not only carrying everything alone.

### Protective lie

> If I can explain the science and keep moving, nobody will notice how scared I am.

### Fear

Failing someone in a way that proves he was never ready. He also fears adults deciding he is either a child to bench or a tool to deploy.

### Voice

Fast, technically curious, self-deprecating, and prone to overexplaining. His jokes can become babble under stress. He is not always the wisest person in the room; his strength is movement, improvisation, and the instinct to save the person in front of him.

### Relationship with Henry

Peter rescues Henry because he recognizes a novice making a mistake, not because a prophecy tells him to. He teaches:

- rescue before pursuit;
- exits and civilians;
- how to leave before police arrive;
- how to use a mask and equipment safely;
- how not to freeze a living person when the weapon or terrain is the real target.

Henry discovers Peter's identity first because Peter is terrible at behaving like someone who is not Spider-Man. This becomes a recurring joke, but Peter's embarrassment must not erase the trust involved.

### Key tensions

- Peter is more experienced, but Henry can be better at cold terrain and thermal problems.
- Peter wants Henry to be cautious but repeatedly underestimates his own risk.
- Peter hides the Civil War recruitment because he thinks protecting Henry means excluding him.
- Peter's memory being erased in *No Way Home* turns the friendship into an absence; rebuilding must be earned.

### Knowledge boundary

Peter learns Henry's power and basic life history early. He learns about Loki in AOU. He does not know the TVA/Sylvie truth until the late story and should not infer it simply because the audience can.

### Failure modes

- lies by omission;
- overpromises safety;
- assumes a clever gadget is a plan;
- tries to make a joke when Henry needs an answer;
- allows Tony's approval to steer him;
- thinks friendship can be protected by withholding information.

### Growth markers

Peter tells Henry the truth before a crisis forces it, lets Henry choose a risk, and later seeks him out after the memory spell without demanding that the old friendship return instantly.

## 3. Elena Reyes

**Role:** Henry's long-term foster guardian and legal adult at home
**Age at opening:** mid-40s
**Work:** hospital billing/records coordinator; practical enough to understand forms, not powerful enough to alter them casually
**Status:** stable, humane, not abusive; Henry's home is not a misery factory

### Surface want

Elena wants Henry fed, housed, in school, and alive. She wants him to have choices she did not have.

### Deeper need

She needs to accept that protecting Henry cannot mean pretending danger is absent or deciding every risk for him.

### Protective lie

> If the household is organized enough, nothing else can take him.

### Fear

A system will classify Henry as a problem and remove him. She has seen enough paperwork to know that a person can become a box checked by someone who never met them.

### Voice and habits

Direct, dry, observant, and not easily impressed by superhero grandeur. She keeps spare towels, checks expiration dates, and knows when Henry is lying because he becomes over-polite. She does not make speeches about being his real mother; she cooks, calls schools, attends appointments, and sets consequences.

### Relationship with Henry

At opening she knows Henry as a guarded teenager with ordinary problems. When powers appear, she is frightened but does not turn him into a monster or a miracle. Her major boundary is honesty about injuries and whereabouts.

### Knowledge progression

- A1: knows Henry is hiding something;
- A2: learns the masked activity or is told after a near miss;
- A3: learns a limited adult file exists;
- A4: learns Loki's identity and the impossible records;
- A11: survives five years without Henry and must meet the returned version of a child who did not experience the gap;
- A15: learns the TVA moved him but refuses to let that erase the home they made.

### Failure mode

She can become controlling when afraid and mistake a demand for honesty for a demand that Henry stop being brave. She must apologize and change the rule when wrong.

### Ending function

Elena proves that the biological reveal does not invalidate human care. She is part of Henry's chosen history, not an obstacle to the Asgardian one.

## 4. Eleanor Cooper

**Role:** Henry's human mother; historical/evidence character
**Born:** 1949, working date
**Age in 1971:** 22
**Location:** Portland/Seattle corridor during the D.B. Cooper period
**Status:** dies in 2008 after an ordinary adult life marked by the unresolved loss of her child

### Surface want

Eleanor wants a life that is stable, private, and hers. She is curious about people who pass through town but does not mistake charm for destiny.

### Deeper need

She needs to decide what to preserve when the person she met may have been lying about every important fact.

### Voice and habits

Observant, grounded, and specific. She records times, weather, objects, and what people actually said because she does not trust memory alone. Her writing is not prophetic. It contains uncertainty, embarrassment, irritation, and practical details.

### Relationship with Loki/Dan

Loki is charming, evasive, funny, and briefly sincere. Eleanor does not know he is Asgardian or that he is D.B. Cooper until the overlap becomes impossible to ignore. Their relationship is short. It should feel like two people making an impulsive choice, not like a cosmic soulmate narrative.

### Key objects

- a notebook with details of the encounter;
- a photograph with a partial face/hand and a distinctive tie or token;
- a small metal token of unknown composition that Loki leaves or loses;
- a later birth/medical document that never reaches Henry through a clean line.

### Knowledge boundary

Eleanor never learns the TVA truth. She knows she lost a child and that the man connected to the pregnancy was not who he said he was. The tragedy is ordinary uncertainty, not secret cosmic exposition.

### Narrative rule

Do not use Eleanor as a flawless mother, damsel, prophecy author, or dead woman whose only function is to make Henry sad. Her papers must contain her life, not just clues.

## 5. Loki — Henry's father

**Role:** biological father; recognizable MCU Loki; later deceased original Loki
**1971 identity:** D.B. Cooper / Dan Cooper while on Earth
**Status at AOU reveal:** alive in Asgardian political circumstances; does not know Henry exists

### Surface want

Loki wants control of the narrative, leverage over others, and the freedom to define himself before someone else does it for him.

### Deeper need

He needs to act without requiring the action to restore his pride, win a contest, or prove that he was right.

### Protective lie

> If I am the one manipulating the relationship, I cannot be humiliated by it.

### Fear

Being irrelevant, exposed as unwanted, or asked for a form of love he cannot perform convincingly. He also fears that Henry will see the worst of him and be correct.

### Voice

Precise, theatrical when useful, cutting when cornered, and capable of quiet sincerity that he immediately tries to disguise. He should sound like MCU Loki, not a sanitized romantic father or an omniscient mastermind.

### 1971 behavior

Loki's D.B. Cooper stunt is still a lost-bet story with Thor. His Earth-side relationship with Eleanor is a brief, human-scale detour. He does not plan a child, leave a prophecy, or return because of destiny. He may remember Eleanor imperfectly and may initially doubt whether Henry's evidence is a trap.

### AOU response

- disbelief;
- suspicion of Asgardian or Avengers manipulation;
- anger at being used as a political weapon;
- curiosity about Henry's mind and powers;
- inappropriate humor;
- a question about Henry's life that sounds like interrogation;
- a second conversation because he cannot stop thinking about the first.

### Relationship with Henry

Loki cannot demand the title “father.” Henry may call him Loki, use no name, or change depending on the scene. Loki's access is limited by Henry, Elena, Thor, Peter, and circumstance.

Early care is indirect:

- returning an object;
- correcting an inaccurate record;
- sending information without asking for gratitude;
- showing up when he could have stayed away;
- refusing to let someone use Henry as a weapon even while claiming not to care.

### Failure modes

- turns a question into a duel;
- offers a clever answer instead of an honest one;
- treats Henry's boundaries as a challenge;
- uses magic to avoid vulnerability;
- mistakes protection for control;
- disappears after a difficult conversation.

### Ending direction

The original Loki dies in *Infinity War* before the relationship is resolved. His death should leave grief and unfinished work, not a neat father-son redemption. The later 2012 variant is not a replacement.

## 6. Thor Odinson

**Role:** Henry's later family relationship; canon Avenger and Asgardian prince/king
**Pre-reveal status:** limited acquaintance only
**Post-reveal status:** gradual brotherly/uncle-like bond

### Surface want

Thor wants to protect people and preserve the bonds he believes can survive betrayal.

### Deeper need

He needs to learn that protection without listening can become another form of control, and that loving Loki does not require him to make Henry absorb Loki's history.

### Protective lie

> If I stand between danger and the person, I have done enough.

### Voice

Grand, earnest, literal in an appealing way, increasingly capable of quiet humor and direct apology. Thor can be socially clumsy without becoming stupid.

### Pre-reveal encounters

- sees Henry in a rescue;
- offers a battlefield correction;
- notices Henry's stubbornness and unusual cold;
- remembers him as a promising but underqualified Midgardian.

No private confessions, no protective attachment, no secret family certainty.

### Post-reveal progression

1. shock and suspicion;
2. concern for a child caught in Loki's history;
3. attempts to help too much;
4. learns Henry wants information, not ceremonial protection;
5. makes space for Henry without claiming him;
6. remains present after Loki's death.

### Failure modes

- speaks for Henry;
- assumes Asgardian answers are automatically better;
- tries to make Henry a bridge to Loki;
- carries grief as if it gives him authority over Henry.

### Ending direction

Thor's relationship with Henry is earned after the reveal and survives the distinction between the original Loki and the TVA variant.

## 7. Sylvie

**Role:** late-story rescuer, temporal fugitive, morally complicated source of Henry's concealment
**First reader appearance:** late TVA phase, unless a deliberately ambiguous fragment is approved
**Author truth:** takes infant Henry during her escape from the TVA

### Surface want

Sylvie wants freedom from systems that claim ownership over her life and the ability to choose her own direction.

### Deeper need

She needs to accept that rescuing someone can still impose a story on them, and that good motives do not erase consequences.

### Protective lie

> If I move first and never let anyone control me, I cannot become what they made me.

### Voice

Blunt, suspicious, fast to anger, allergic to ceremonial authority. Her care is practical and sometimes abrupt. She does not narrate herself as a saint.

### Infant rescue

During a TVA capture operation involving Henry, Sylvie sees an infant being treated as a file and remembers her own childhood. She takes him in the chaos. She does not know how to raise him, does not create a grand plan, and chooses a low-visibility Sacred Timeline insertion point in 2000.

She uses concealment enchantment to hide Jotun/Asgardian origin and dampen temporal detection. She does not erase an adult person's memories or design Henry's personality.

### Relationship with Henry

Henry is grateful for the survival and angry about the secrecy. Sylvie must tolerate both. Their conversations should include practical disagreement, not instant absolution.

### Failure modes

- refuses to explain because explanation feels like surrender;
- treats Henry's anger as a threat;
- believes the fact that she saved him ends the moral question;
- overcorrects by withholding more information.

### Ending direction

She remains responsible for the choice without becoming a villain or perfect mother figure.

## 8. Loki variant from 2012

**Role:** TVA-era parallel to Henry's father; information source and separate character
**Rule:** not Henry's father, not an emotional replacement, not a shortcut to forgiveness

### Function

The variant helps distinguish identity from continuity. He can understand Loki's habits and offer context, but he did not meet Eleanor, did not learn about Henry in the original line, and did not die in the same way.

### Voice

Recognizably Loki, but altered by TVA experience, future knowledge, and responsibility for branches. He remains vain, observant, and capable of growth.

### Relationship with Henry

Initial curiosity, discomfort, and a careful attempt not to claim a relationship that belongs to another version of himself. He may help Henry read a file because he recognizes the danger of being turned into one.

### Failure mode

He can speak as if insight into the original Loki gives him authority. Henry corrects him.

## 9. Tony Stark

**Role:** adult inventor, Avengers leader/pressure source, Peter's canon recruiter
**Core want:** solve danger through invention and control
**Core need:** accept that not every risk can be engineered away, especially when the person involved is a child with agency
**Voice:** fast, funny, defensive, perceptive, and capable of cruelty when frightened

### Henry relationship

Tony sees a thermal system, a liability, and a child who resembles several dangerous patterns. He may offer equipment or testing, but he does not become Henry's father substitute. After AOU, Tony's guilt and Ultron experience make him more cautious and more controlling at the same time.

### Failure modes

- offers a solution before asking what Henry wants;
- treats consent as a technical inconvenience;
- compares Henry to Peter in ways that create resentment;
- assumes “I am trying to protect you” ends a conversation.

### Canon protection

Tony remains the person whose actions lead to Ultron and who recruits Peter in *Civil War*. Henry does not prevent the canon arc.

---

## 10. Steve Rogers

**Role:** moral counterweight, adult boundary model, Civil War pressure
**Core want:** protect people from systems that claim authority without accountability
**Core need:** recognize that his ideals can also ask young people to carry adult conflicts
**Voice:** plain, sincere, dry when relaxed, uncomfortable with manipulation

### Henry relationship

Steve is one of the first adults to ask Henry what he wants without assuming the answer. He still has blind spots about secrecy and danger. He tells Henry that refusing a mission is not cowardice.

### Failure mode

He can make a principled choice on Henry's behalf and only realize afterward that the principle did not include listening.

---

## 11. Natasha Romanoff

**Role:** observer, intelligence analyst, practical adult contact
**Core want:** identify threats before they become disasters
**Core need:** allow people to be more than patterns she can predict
**Voice:** economical, dry, controlled, rarely wastes a question

### Henry relationship

Natasha notices that Henry counts exits, hides injuries, and lies differently when he is scared. She does not expose him for sport. She helps the Avengers establish safer rules and is part of the evidence chain without becoming a secret all-knowing detective.

### Failure mode

She tests people past the point where they feel tested and mistakes information for trust.

---

## 12. Bruce Banner / the Hulk

**Role:** medical and scientific limit-setter; AOU core canon character
**Core want:** understand dangerous transformations and prevent harm
**Core need:** accept that understanding is not the same as control
**Voice:** gentle, precise, self-deprecating, increasingly strained under pressure

### Henry relationship

Bruce explains dehydration, heat exchange, tissue damage, and the danger of freezing his own body. He refuses to turn Henry into a lab sample. His technical observations support the AOU evidence chain but do not alone identify Loki.

### Failure mode

He can retreat into explanation when Henry needs a human answer.

---

## 13. Clint Barton

**Role:** practical field adult and family-centered counterweight
**Core want:** get people home alive
**Voice:** understated, dry, direct

Clint is useful in small scenes: he asks whether Henry has eaten, makes him practice exit plans, and refuses to romanticize hero work. His farmhouse/family role remains independent. He does not adopt Henry in one conversation.

---

## 14. Wanda Maximoff

**Role:** AOU/Civil War/Avengers character whose powers and grief remain her own
**Core want:** protect the people she chooses and make meaning from loss
**Core need:** face the difference between control and safety
**Voice:** initially guarded and intense; later more deliberate

### Henry relationship

Wanda may sense emotional pressure around Henry or see an image connected to cold/records, but she is not used as a paternity scanner. Henry understands that her power can reveal fear without revealing truth.

### Canon protection

Her AOU relationship with Pietro, manipulation by Ultron, and later grief remain central to her story.

---

## 15. Pietro Maximoff

**Role:** AOU independent character and pressure point for speed versus control
**Voice:** fast, teasing, impatient, protective of Wanda

Pietro can challenge Henry's movement assumptions and joke about ice being slower than he is. He is not turned into Henry's best friend before his canon death. His sacrifice remains his.

---

## 16. Vision

**Role:** AOU/Avengers nonhuman perspective on identity and records
**Core question:** what makes a person more than data?
**Voice:** calm, literal, curious, increasingly humane

Vision can detect that Henry's biological data is inconsistent without knowing why. He must not solve the TVA mystery. His existence gives Henry a useful contrast: a being made through records and power who still develops a self through choices.

---

## 17. Bucky Barnes and Sam Wilson

**Roles:** Civil War and post-Civil War canon anchors

- **Bucky:** Henry sees someone whose identity has been treated as a file and weapon, but does not presume to understand him.
- **Sam:** practical, socially perceptive, willing to call out adult hypocrisy; can tell Henry that “not choosing a side” still creates responsibilities.

Neither is absorbed into Henry's family plot.

---

## 18. Stephen Strange and Wong

### Stephen Strange

**Role:** later rules expert, not a universal diagnostician
**Voice:** arrogant, fast, precise, increasingly responsible

Strange can identify that a concealment effect is layered or nonlocal. He cannot simply remove it without understanding the consequences and must not solve the age mystery before the TVA phase.

### Wong

**Role:** practical guardian of magical procedure
**Voice:** dry, firm, less impressed by Strange's performance

Wong helps establish that magical records and concealment have costs and boundaries.

## 19. Peter's school circle

### Ned Leeds

Tech-minded, enthusiastic, socially warmer than Peter, and more observant than people assume. He is not reduced to a secret-keeper machine. He can notice that Henry and Peter disappear at similar times without immediately understanding why.

### Michelle / MJ

Dry, perceptive, and not obligated to become the boys' emotional therapist. She sees through social performances and can notice the difference between Henry's quiet and Peter's panic.

### Liz Allen

A real classmate with interests and pressures outside the hero plot. Her Homecoming role remains recognizable.

### Flash Thompson

A source of social friction who is allowed to grow and be complicated. He should not be an endlessly humiliated prop.

### Aunt May

Peter's home adult. She is caring, alert, and increasingly aware that Peter's ordinary life is not ordinary. Her concern should parallel Elena's without making the two households identical.

---

## 20. Valkyrie / Hela / Asgardian supporting cast

### Valkyrie

Independent survivor, fighter, and leader. She does not become Henry's warrior trainer by default. Her expertise can expose the difference between Asgardian culture and Loki's personal history.

### Hela

The canon destructive force of *Ragnarok*. Henry does not become the hidden answer to her. Her threat demonstrates that heritage does not grant Henry immunity or status.

### Heimdall

If used, he is a limited witness or communication point, not a magic camera that knows Henry's entire truth. His perception can be obstructed by Sylvie's concealment.

### Frigga and Odin

Their canon relationships to Loki and Thor remain intact. Henry does not become the retroactive center of their family history. If Henry encounters memories or records of them, they should complicate rather than simplify his understanding of Loki.

## 21. Antagonist bibles

### Ultron

**Function:** AOU's central antagonist.
**Rule:** Henry is a pressure point and witness, not the reason Ultron awakens or the person who defeats him.

Ultron can identify Henry as a vulnerable biological system and exploit his records, but he must not know the TVA truth. His contempt for human inconsistency can contrast with Henry's insistence that imperfect records do not make people disposable.

### Helmut Zemo

**Function:** Civil War's strategic antagonist.
**Rule:** Zemo's grief and plan remain focused on the Avengers and Bucky. Henry may understand the danger of weaponized records, but he does not solve Zemo.

### Adrian Toomes / Vulture

**Function:** Homecoming's local antagonist.
**Rule:** Peter owns the confrontation. Henry may help with evacuation, equipment reading, or civilian safety, but not identify and defeat Toomes for Peter.

### Thanos

**Function:** Infinity War/Endgame cosmic antagonist.
**Rule:** Henry's power cannot defeat him. Henry's contribution is rescue, survival, and a meaningful choice under impossible scale.

### Wanda's later antagonistic pressure

Wanda is not made Henry's villain. If Henry intersects with *WandaVision* or *Multiverse of Madness*, the focus is on the consequences of grief and reality manipulation, not on making Wanda responsible for Henry's origin.

### TVA bureaucracy

The TVA is a system-level antagonist before it is a cast of individual villains. Its files are neat, its categories are confident, and its assumptions are wrong or incomplete. Individual TVA workers may be compassionate, fearful, or cruel without turning the institution into a simple good/evil binary.

### Renslayer / TVA leadership

Use as an institutional pressure point and keeper of selective records. Do not make her secretly responsible for every event in Henry's life.
