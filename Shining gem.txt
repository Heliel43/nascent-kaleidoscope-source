Prologue ​


In the midst of an endless void, I found myself lost, adrift in a sea of nothingness. Time seemed irrelevant, a concept without meaning in this desolate expanse.

I toyed with the idea of measuring time, foolishly attempting to count the seconds, but the futility of such a task became painfully apparent. Each moment blurred into the next, a relentless stream that threatened to overwhelm my senses.

Lesson learned: don't try to quantify the infinite when time itself refuses to be tethered, in another words, don't try to count numbers when time is not present to stop you.

Anyway, I don't know why I am here, for what purpose, I didn't know. It could have been CYOA I filled, it could have been a Waifu Catalog build I made, for whatever it was one thing is certain, my dumbass definitely picked something outrageously dangerous just for boobs.

"Please spare me the mention of boobs, partner. I've only just escaped a rather... delicate situation involving... well, you know."

As I pondered my purpose in this void, a voice interrupted my thoughts, booming with a resonance that reverberated through my very soul.

'Boobie situation? Nevermind that. Who are you? And please assure me you're not here to make a meal out of me,' I quipped, my unease mounting in the presence of an unfamiliar entity. A little more than concerned since usually there are no nice creatures in void. Especially those outer beings.

"Don't lump me in with those abominations, kid. I may be a dragon, but I have standards. And trust me, humans aren't as delectable as they think they are. Yuck, one time I ate one of the princesses to see what all that fuss was about and I got constipation for the whole month."

Ouch. Mental note: avoid becoming dragon cuisine for both our sake. So, any chance we're escaping this void anytime soon?

"You'll find out soon enough. For now, just think of me as your trusty isekai companion. And as for our location? Well, congratulations, we've arrived at your destination. NOW WAKE UP!"

With a deafening roar, the dragon breathed fire in my way, feeling of burning flesh and melting bones jolted me awake, my heart pounding against my chest as I struggled to regain my bearings. Blinking away the remnants of my dream, I found myself... not myself.

I awoke to a new reality, one filled with memories and emotions foreign to me. Depression and arrogance seeped into my consciousness, alien sentiments that left me reeling in confusion.




I awoke in a workshop, surrounded by glittering gems and ancient tomes. The opulence of my surroundings sent a thrill of greed coursing through me, a sensation I shamelessly indulged in.

"It's all mine now," I chuckled to myself, my grin widening at the sight of such wealth.

There was no resistance within me, no sense of displacement or unease. I simply existed in this new reality, accepting it with a resigned sigh.

Observing Rin Tohsaka's adventures from the comfort of my screen had always been entertaining, but inhabiting his role was a far cry from the excitement I had imagined.

Especially in this chaotic hell known as the Nasuverse.

And yes, I say "him," thankfully spared the indignity of a gender swap and the tampering of Rin's history and physique before being thrust into this world.

And yes, I'm still a mage, poised to participate in the Holy Grail War, with the course of history mirroring that of the original timeline, save for Rin's gender. And, oh joy, Shirou is now a girl. How utterly cliché.

But at least past Rin had the foresight to safeguard his family's fortune, refusing to blindly entrust it to that charlatan of a priest.

Fuck you Kirei.

I scolded myself with a light slap to the cheeks, shaking off the disorientation that lingered from my awakening in the workshop. Taking a moment to survey my surroundings, I made my way out into the unknown.

So, let's recap: I somehow ended up in the Void, probably after kicking the bucket, had a delightful encounter with a constipated dragon who had a taste for royalty, and there was definitely some fiery theatrics involved. Fun times.

Then came the merger with Rin Tohsaka, seamless and oddly perfect. Memories from both lives flooded my mind, mingling together like a mixed tape of experiences. Skills and quirks collided, resulting in a mishmash of traits that left me feeling like a patched-together Frankenstein's monster of personalities.

Speaking of personalities, it seemed my own stubborn demeanor had muscled its way to the forefront, overshadowing Rin's original disposition. Not that Rin was a ray of sunshine to begin with—what with the whole dead parents and estranged sister situation—but my dominant traits of sarcasm and skepticism seemed to have won the battle for control.

Perhaps Rin was simply too spineless to put up a fight, relinquishing control without so much as a whimper. Or maybe he was just relieved to have someone else take the reins. Either way, the end result was the same: I was stuck wearing Rin Tohsaka's shoes, whether they fit comfortably or not.

Well, here I am, trapped in the unpredictable world of the Nasuverse, where every corner hides a potential threat and trust is a luxury one can ill afford.

Best-case scenario? This turns out to be one of those heartwarming tales like "Today's Menu in the Emiya Household," where we bond over cooking mishaps and existential crises.

Worst-case scenario? We're plunged headfirst into the fiery abyss of singularity F in FGO, where chaos reigns and the city burns with the intensity of a thousand suns.

I glanced down at my clenched fist, the familiar sight of a crimson tattoo serving as a stark reminder of the trials that lay ahead. With a resigned sigh, I steeled myself for whatever fate had in store.

The clock on the wall stubbornly ticked away the seconds, its hands inching closer to midnight. Or was it? Who was I kidding? It was already well past 1 AM. Looks like I've landed myself smack dab at the genesis of this whole catastrophe, haven't I?

With a resigned sigh, I accepted my fate. I had no choice but to toe the line of canon, because there are already Servants, heroes and legends of the past roaming around the city.

And then there's me, heir to a legacy steeped in chaos, second owner of a city that sounds like it was ripped straight from the title screen of a risqué video game. Not that it granted me any real authority over the place, except when it came to matters of magecraft.

And in this forgotten corner of the city, magecraft was about as useful as a waterproof teabag.

Ugh. Let's just get this over with. I trudged back to the workshop, fingers curling around the crimson crystal my father had bestowed upon me before meeting his untimely demise a decade ago.

This little trinket had been quietly hoarding mana for the past ten years, waiting for its moment to shine. A mana nuke in a conveniently portable package.

Closing my eyes, I attempted to open my magic circuit, only to be met with stubborn resistance. Ah, the joys of merging with another consciousness. Apparently, my mental triggers had been reshuffled in the process.

With a mental eye-roll, I conjured up the image of that constipated dragon unleashing a torrent of flames in my direction. The sensation of searing heat washed over me, burning my flesh, melting my bones, my magic circuit springing to life in response along with surprised and pained gasp.

Fine, I'll leave it up to Fate, unless it's some useless Servant who can't fight. I need someone with an arsenal of Noble Phantasms, whether they're a king or a faker. Thankfully I have catalyst for that, even if its not hundred percent chance of success.

The energy required for the summoning is sustained by the Grail, so I don't have to worry about that. Plus, I don't need to recite the entire summoning chant since I already possess a command seal.

No need to stick to the textbook method like canon Rin. At least not entirely.

But dammit, I can't resist. I won't get another chance to do this.

"Heed my words, my will creates your body, and your sword creates my destiny," I chanted, opening my heart and mind. The magic circle hummed with power, the leyline beneath the mansion clicking into place and providing an extra surge of mana for the ritual.

"f you heed the Grail's cause and obey my will and reason, then answer my summoning."

Despite being in the locked basement of my workshop, the wind whipped around me, swirling with an otherworldly force.

"I hereby swear that I shall be all the good in this world, and I shall defeat all the evil in this world."

My hand burned, the tattoo-like mark engraved on back of my palm igniting and merging with my soul.

"Seventh Heaven clad in great word of Power, come forth from the circle of binding, GUARDIAN OF THE SCALE!"

The tattoo that had invaded my soul shot out like a chain, tearing through space and time to pierce another soul and bring it into this world as a Servant.

Thus, I, a mere mortal, a mage, a lower existence, was granted the title of Master, albeit with a chain that put me above the higher existence, signifying my authority over them.

BOOM!

Suddenly, the magic circle shut down, its purpose fulfilled. At first glance, there was nothing inside the circle, but a loud crashing sound echoed from the living room, suggesting otherwise.

I bolted out of the workshop, fueled by the rush of mana coursing through my legs and spine, leaping ahead in giant staircase of Tohsaka mansion, propelling me faster than any normal human. With a swift kick, I sent the living room door crashing down, my eyes fixated on the concentrated mass of mana in one spot.

There she sat, a beautiful and exotic woman in her late twenties, perched atop my toppled cupboards as if they were her throne.

"Oh my, and what makes you think I'll just casually obey and fight at your behest?" she questioned, her smug tone still evident as she propped her legs up on the still-standing table despite the wreckage.
