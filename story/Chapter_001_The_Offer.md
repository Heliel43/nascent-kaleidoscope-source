# Chapter 1: The Offer You Can't Refuse

I slowly opened my eyes, only to be met by an unfamiliar ceiling and the sensation of a different mattress.

It felt like I had foreign thoughts in my head. I don't know when it started, sometime recently, but I would catch myself thinking of something I had no idea about, in too vivid a recollection to think them mere daydreams. People talking around me, I heard my teacher lecture about something irrelevant, but my mind was completely unfocused.

No, that wasn't right. That was before. The lab. The pressure in my chest.

I rose from the bed and looked around to see that I was in some sort of empty room with no windows or doors.

"Where the hell am I?" I looked at my hand and clenched it and then unclenched it. It was unnaturally cold. "Something doesn't feel right... Am I... dead?"

You would be correct.

"Gah!" I got spooked by the sudden voice and jumped off the bed. "Wh- Who said that!?"

I did.

The voice was coming from everywhere, even in my own head. "Who are you? What are you? What happened to me? Where is this place!?"

In that order: I do not have a name, I am simply an A.I. created for the sole purpose of helping you in your transition to your new life. You have suffered a heart attack while you were sleeping, your parents found you this morning and took you to the hospital where you were pronounced dead. Finally, you are in the nexus where the earth and the afterlife meet. You were brought here because you were chosen.

My face fell at the news. The lab, the late nights, the energy drinks. It tracked.

"Oh, so I really am dead..."

You seem... awfully calm about this, considering the situation.

"Would it be better if I was kicking, screaming, and cursing? It wouldn't bring me back to life. Of course, not counting the shock that's keeping me calm right now." I explained.

I was in the middle of class when I couldn't help but just stare at the ceiling. Even as everyone got up and left, I was still stuck in a daze. Was I going crazy? I knew what burnout was, but didn't that come on gradually, not just out of the blue?

As amusing as it would be to watch that happening, I'd rather get this along.

"Get what-"

Congratulations! You have been chosen to be the new host for the Gamer System.

I blinked at the reveal. "Gamer System? You mean that gimmick in most fanfictions where the OC gets all these broken skills and abilities?"

Correct. I will guide you through the process so that you can get acquainted with the system.

"Alright, let's get started." A screen suddenly popped in front of me.

```
Quest Alert!
Quest: Tutorial
Pretty self explanatory, yes?
Objective 1: Open Status Menu
Objective 2: Open Inventory
Objective 3: Create your character
Rewards: 1000 EXP, Skills, Perks, ?
```

"So my first quest is a tutorial quest." I looked back and forth. "Um... how do I get started?"

The first step is to open your menu. Simply make a mental command or say "Status"

"Status." Immediately a long screen appeared in front of me with information.

```

```

I was completely in shock, this was real. This was an actual status screen. A REAL status screen.

"Why doesn't it say my name? Or my race for that matter?"

All new users must register under a new name and race. If you use your original name, you will experience HELL. Please keep in mind that we are not responsible for any permanent harm that comes to you.

Well, that was one way of saying we don't give a damn about your life choices.

The next step is your inventory. Either tap on the bag icon on the screen or simply think "Inventory"

I did just that and the window quickly changed. It now showed a series of empty boxes that hold nothing except for two highlighted ones that have my current clothes. On the left of the screen shows what I have equipped right now.

"This is not too bad." I said as I scrolled down on the page.

```
Please select a race:
[Human] [?] [?] [?]
```

"One option is visible, while the other three are unknown. What's that about?"

The first icon is self explanatory.
Second is for low-tier entities: Slime, Goblin, Skeleton and so on.
The third is for mid-tier entities: Demi-Human, Dragons, Devils, Angels, etc.
The last one is for high-tier races: Dragon God, Primarch, Outer God, Demon God, High Seraphim, etc.
These three choices are completely random, so choose on your own merit.

By its logic, humans would be considered a mid-tier race. I could choose Human, or try my luck with the other three. I didn't really like taking risks, especially when they're luck based; my luck sucks.

Despite how tempting it was, I was going to play it safe and go with [Human]. I wanted something grounded. Something that could learn.

Race selected: Human

A universal race, existing across all of time and space. Despite their organic and overall weak appearance, they make up for it with their wits, tenacity, and overall stubbornness. A jack of all trades. They are creatures of great compassion and great violence. Their true potential, however, lies in their ability to learn and understand, as the strongest and most intelligent of the species can turn the tables on the most powerful of opponents.

Race stats:
Vit x 2
STR x 4
DEX x 3
END x 4
INT x 3
WIS x 3
MAG x 2
LUK x 2
CHA x 1

I did not expect there to be racial stat bonuses. This could be helpful, especially when I was powerful. Though I still had doubts, at least I knew what I was dealing with.

Lastly is character creation. This is where you will create the body that will house your soul for the foreseeable future. You will be reborn into the world you've chosen, during that time a pseudo-personality will protect it until you awaken.

I nodded and the screen changed to show the diagram of a human body alongside several icons that will allow for customization. After a few minutes, I closed the window satisfied with the change. I didn't want to look like a model. I wanted to look like someone who could survive. Lean, early twenties, brown hair, scar on left hand from lab work. Nothing flashy.

Is this the look you desire?
[Y/N]

I pressed "Yes".

Now it is time to type your new name.

I put in the first name I could think of. Something simple. Something that wasn't my old name.

Kael Thorne. Is this correct?
[Y/N]

I pressed "Yes".

Well... that's going to be a problem. There aren't a lot of things that piss me off. Annoyed, yes, but not angry. It's going to be a bitch to handle and the fact that it shuts off [Gamer's Mind] doesn't make me feel any better. I needed to keep that in check.

"So now that that's over, what else is there left to do?"

All that is left now is to select which universe you desire to reincarnate in.

Choose the Universe you want to go:
[Aelindra - Fractured Frontier]

A world of dungeons, guilds, and slow gods. Magic is real but not free. Progression is earned. Death is permanent for most.

I didn't even hesitate.

Are you ready?

I nodded. A hissing noise could be heard and when I turned, a section of the wall broke down revealing a blinding light. I covered my eyes as I approached the light, it was so blinding I could go blind if I even dared to open them. I entered and vanished into the portal, not long the wall began to repair itself, restoring it to what it was before.

[Game Start!]
[Save? Y/N]

I pressed Yes without thinking. Then the light took me.
