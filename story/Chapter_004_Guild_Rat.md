# Chapter 4: Guild Rat

The adventurer's guild was a grand building, eye-catching in the peculiar way that government buildings were. Stately, was the word people often used. Big and officious, to intimidate as much as impress. It was two stories, stone and timber, with a watchtower covered in - I squinted my eyes - notice boards?

"You'll be wanting the adventurer's guild. Take a turn at the next intersection, and it'd be near the end of the street. Look for that tower," Lyra had said that morning, pointing me down the main drag of Lornswatch.

"Adventurer's guild? Not the farmer's association or something?" I had asked, still half-asleep on the straw pallet Rowan had let me use for a copper.

She had given me a look. "You really did hit your head."

Now I was standing in front of it. My rusted sword was at my hip, my clothes were still blood-stained but washed as best as I could. HP was full - 850/850. That still felt weird to think.

Inside, it was busy for a small town. A few rough-looking men and women at tables, counting coins, arguing over a map. The smell of ink and old wood. And at the far end, a counter with a receptionist.

The wavy-haired employee coughed into a fist to draw my gaze, before giving me a gentle smile. "Welcome to the adventurer's guild. My name is Mira, a guild receptionist. How may I help you today?"

"Kael Thorne. I was... I was part of Joren's team on the east trail. I need to report."

Her smile faded a little, professional sympathy sliding into place. "Ah. We heard. Lyra Quinn brought you in last night. The Guildmaster wants to see you, but first -"

She retrieved a rectangular sheet - each was a bit bigger than a person's hand - stored below the counter, passing one to me. "Your adventurer card. Please fill it out now, then tap the bottom left corner. Once set, a card is linked by magic to its owner, and will be very important in your line of work as it allows you to choose a job class -"

I stared at the card. It was blank, with faint lines for name, age, class.

"I'm already registered, I think. The previous - I mean, I was registered before."

"Then we'll need to re-issue. Your old card was with Joren's team. Lost, presumably." She slid an ink pen across. "Name, age, intended class."

I filled it: Kael Thorne, 19, Class: Undecided.

I tapped the corner. The card glowed faintly, warm under my thumb. Text etched itself.

```
[Adventurer Card]
Name: Kael Thorne
Rank: F (Lowest)
Level: 6
Class: None
Status: Active
Debt: 2 Silver (Guide Fee - Lyra Quinn)
```

"Wonderful!" Mira exclaimed, though her eyes were on the Debt line. "Then let me be the first to welcome you back to the adventurer's guild. As you are F-rank, you are considered a full-fledged adventurer by the guild and are allowed to take F and E rank jobs."

"Never mess with adventurer's guild receptionists in general," someone muttered behind me. I ignored it.

"Guildmaster?" I asked.

"Second door on the left. He is expecting you."

The Guildmaster was not what I expected. I expected a huge, scarred warrior. Instead, he was a thin man in his fifties, with spectacles and a ledger that looked heavier than he was.

"Kael Thorne," he said without looking up. "Sit."

I sat.

"Report."

I gave him the edited version. Woke up, wolves, alpha, killed one, found dead teammates, Lyra found me.

He listened, pen scratching.

"Which is why I was currently in the Adventurer's Guild early in the morning, standing right in front of the receptionist's desk with a blank look on my face," I almost said out loud, but caught myself. That was someone else's line.

"It has also come to our attention that you are not a registered adventurer here," he said, finally looking up. "Well, you were, but under Joren's party. Joren-sama had mentioned that you are an adventurer in someplace else, and normally these sorts of deeds result in promotions. As such, we're also giving you the opportunity for you to join our guild with a rank of Gold -"

He stopped, saw my face, and corrected himself.

"No, sorry, wrong script. F-rank. You remain F-rank. But you survived an alpha at Level 5. That's... notable. Most F-ranks die to juveniles."

"I had help. Muscle memory. And luck."

"Adventurer Guilds are pretty useful. This place, along with taverns, are the best places to gain information based on my experience," he said, as if quoting a guidebook. "However, that also applies in relation to my line of work now, especially now that I'm building a support base for myself here."

He closed his ledger.

"Here's what happens now, Kael. You owe Lyra two silver. You owe the guild one silver for card re-issue. You have zero. You have a rusted sword worth maybe three coppers. You have two wolf fangs worth ten coppers total if Mira is feeling generous."

He leaned forward.

"So you will take a job. Today. Something simple. Herb gathering, or ditch cleaning. You will earn, you will pay your debts, and you will train. Because if you go out again like you are now, you will die, and I will have to write another letter to a family that doesn't exist."

It was blunt. Pragmatic. I liked it.

"Understood."

"Good. Mira has a list. And Kael -"

"Yes?"

"Go see the healer first. You may be at full HP, whatever that means for you, but you smell like a corpse."

I left his office. Mira was already holding a small board.

"Herb gathering: Sunpetal, 5 stalks, east field, 8 copper. Ditch cleaning: South road, 6 copper. Rat clearing: Tavern cellar, 10 copper and a meal."

Rat clearing sounded like XP.

"I'll take rats."

She stamped my card. "Accepted. Rowan's tavern. Don't kill his cat."

I headed back to the tavern. Lyra was there, eating breakfast.

"Guild rat now, huh?" she said, seeing the card in my hand.

"Officially."

"Welcome to the bottom. The view's terrible, but the pay's worse."

She pushed a second bowl of stew toward me. "On me. Consider it an investment. You can't pay me back if you starve."

```
Quest Alert!
Quest: Welcome to Lornswatch!
Your body may belong to an adventurer but not the soul. Find your footing and try not to get yourself killed.
Objective 1: Complete 3 F-rank quests
Objective 2: Pay off debt (3 Silver)
Objective 3: Unlock your Mana
Rewards: 1500 EXP, 640 Credits, Skill: [Mana Control LVL1], Rank Up to E
```

That was more like it. A real tutorial.

I sat down and started eating. Lyra watched me for a moment, then went back to her own food.

"You know," she said casually, "most people who survive a party wipe quit. Go back to farming."

"I'm not most people," I said, then winced at how edgy that sounded. "I mean - I don't have a farm to go back to. This is what I've got."

She nodded, as if that was a good enough reason. For her, it probably was.

"Pragmatic," she said again.

"That's the plan."

After breakfast, I went to the cellar. Rats, it turned out, were not normal rats. They were the size of small dogs, with faintly glowing eyes.

I used Observe.

```
[Giant Cave Rat]
Level: 2
HP: 30
Description: Pest. Bites can cause infection. Weak to blunt trauma.
```

I had a rusted sword and Gamer's Body. Let's see how this went.
