# Chapter 5: Grinding

"T-Two." I grunted as I forced myself up. Pushups were not easy, and my arms were still jello, but it was really good experience.

*100 Body XP Gained.* No, not Body XP. SP.

Actually, the System did give me something.

```
+1 STR EXP (Pushup)
```

Turns out I was now strong enough to do basic exercises, and while lifting my arms and working my incredibly weak body did give me XP after a while of doing it, actual direct exercise was a whole other class.

The first time I did an actual pushup I gained XP every rep. Of course I could only do one rep at a time at the start.

But with the ability to go to sleep and wake up fully recovered. I was cheating while Lyra was out on a job. Normally the amount of muscle pain I would be in would put me out of commission for days. If not more considering how weak I still was.

But with a nap I was back and ready to push myself again. Turns out I could actually adjust how much time I slept for as well. There was an actual 24 hour clock when I decided to sleep. I just hadn't seen it because I don't usually keep my eyes open when trying to sleep.

Stupid weird Gamer system.

"T-Threeee!" I grunted, managing a third pushup but it left me on my stomach gasping my arms burning and I realized they weren't going to be any help getting me back on my feet.

"Okay I'm out." I whispered as I simply rested on the floor of the ID's Empty Field.

Damn, when was the last time I cleaned this place? I grumbled being able to look at the ground. Right, it was an ID. It was always clean. That was the point.

I had discovered something crucial yesterday after killing three giant rats.

```
You have killed Giant Cave Rat x3! 45 EXP!
Loot: [Rat Tail x3] [Copper x2]
Quest Objective: Rat clearing - Complete!
Reward: 10 Copper + Meal
```

Rowan had given me the meal - more stew - and Mira had given me 10 copper. I was still in debt, but less.

More importantly, I had discovered ID Create could be used to grind without risking my life or breaking the local economy. The rats in the tavern cellar didn't respawn. The wolves in the ID did.

[ID Create: Forest - Easy]

The world shifted. I was back in a forest, but this one was different - sparser, with three wolves already growling at the edge.

I drew my rusted sword. It was still at 22 durability, but in ID, durability didn't drop. Or it reset on escape. Another cheat.

The first wolf lunged. I dodge rolled - Muscle Memory kicking in - and slashed.

-12 HP to wolf.

It wasn't perfect. Obviously 'mom' had intended for there to be actual lessons on fighting interspersed with the guide. But it was still useful, and I could see my experience going up.

The wolf died.

```
You have killed Forest Wolf LVL 2! 20 EXP!
```

Second wolf. Same.

Third wolf.

After the third, a faint chime.

```
Skill Level Up!
[Short Sword Proficiency LVL 1 -> LVL 2]
```

I nodded pleased. It was slower than leveling Body. Probably because I wasn't actively doing anything, but it was still fascinating. If someone offered you a verbal and text primer on being able to access any weapon around you to control wouldn't you want to learn it?

I kept going. Wolf after wolf. Every five wolves, a slightly bigger one.

*100 Body Experience Gained.* No - *50 Sword EXP Gained.*

—-Body Leveled up! No, Skill Leveled up!

I blinked, breaking my concentration on my failed attempts at squats to stare at the alert.

I quickly threw the alert to the side and opened my stats.

"Short Sword level 2. I'm actually not terrible again." I muttered as I rose up my body was burning and I was sweaty, but this time as I rose up.

My body didn't wobble. I didn't feel weak other than normal exercise tiredness. I started walking around the small ID field and it didn't tire me out, or make me feel like I should stay close to the wall.

I breathed in deeply and let it out. I felt… Normal.

Well… I was hungry. I headed into my mental menu and checked my inventory. The wolf fangs were still there. The rat tails were there. I had 12 copper.

I escaped the ID.

[ID: Escape]
[Rewards: 180 EXP, Wolf Pelt x2]

I was back in the real world, in the small room Rowan let me rent for 2 copper a night. It was spartan. A bed, a nightstand, a door to a tiny bathroom.

Everything but the bed was ignored as I flopped into it with zero grace. I groaned in relief and let my tired body sag into the mattress. Sleep rapidly overtook me and I shut my eyes, but just before I could fully pass out, something shook the bed.

No, nothing shook the bed. That was another story. Here, I was alone.

I chose Sleep: 2 hours.

The System timer popped up. I slept.

I woke up feeling great and ready to start my day. Fully restored. HP, MP, SP full. No muscle pain.

This was broken. And I was going to abuse the hell out of it, but logically. Not 24/7 grinding like an idiot. Structured.

Morning: Real world quests for money and reputation.
Afternoon: ID grinding for skills and EXP.
Evening: Study. I needed to understand mana.

Which brings me to today's problem: Mana.

Mira said the Guild had a basic manual for unlocking mana. Cost: 1 silver. I had 12 copper. I needed 88 more.

Lyra found me at breakfast, again.

"You look less like a corpse," she said. "You been training?"

"Pushups. And wolves."

"Wolves?"

"In my head. Meditation."

She gave me a look that said she didn't believe me, but she didn't push. "Good. You need it. I'm taking a herb job east. Want to come? You can carry and learn. Pay is split 70/30."

"70 for you?"

"I'm Level 12, you're Level 6. And I'm the one who knows what Sunpetal looks like. Yes, 70 for me."

Pragmatic. I liked it.

"Deal."

We headed east, back toward where I had died the first time. The forest was quieter in daylight. Lyra moved like she belonged, pointing out things.

"That's Ironbark. Hard wood, poor kindling," she said, echoing my Observe.

"That's Sunpetal. Yellow, five petals, smells like pepper. Don't pick the white one, that's Fool's Petal, gives you rash."

I used Observe on everything, leveling it slowly.

```
[Sunpetal]
Description: Common alchemical ingredient. Mild mana restorative.
Value: 2 copper per stalk
```

We gathered five stalks. Then ten. Lyra was fast.

"How do you unlock mana?" I asked as we walked.

"You don't 'unlock' it. You feel it. Everyone has it, but most can't use it consciously until they train. Guild manual helps, but real way is to almost die and have your body force it out. Or meditate for a month."

"Which did you do?"

"Both. Almost died to a boar, then meditated for a month while my leg healed." She showed me a faint scar on her calf. "Now I can do this."

She held out her hand. A faint green glow, like wind, swirled around her fingers, then faded.

"That's my aura. Scout's aura. Makes me faster, quieter. You'll get something similar if you train."

"Any tips?"

"Stop thinking of HP and MP as numbers. Think of them as... breathing. You don't think about breathing, you just do it. Mana is same. When you swung that sword yesterday, you used SP without thinking. Mana is same, but deeper."

That was surprisingly helpful. And it matched what I had read in the theoretical books from another story: magic was like a muscle. You could train your channels to grow as you grow up.

We returned to town by noon. Mira paid us: 16 copper total. My share: 5 copper (30% of 16 is 4.8, she rounded up).

"Generous," I said.

"Don't get used to it."

I now had 17 copper. Still far from 1 silver (100 copper).

I went back to rats. The tavern cellar had more - Rowan said they kept coming from a hole in the wall.

```
[Giant Cave Rat]
Level: 2
HP: 30
```

I killed six more. No longer scared. Just efficient. Stab, twist, dodge.

```
Level Up!
Level 7!
5 Stat Points Gained!
```

I put them into VIT and END. Survivability first. My inner academic wanted INT, but dead academics publish nothing.

```
Name: Kael Thorne
Level: 7
HP: 950
MP: 650
SP: 580
VIT: 23
STR: 30
DEX: 25
END: 32
INT: 25
WIS: 25
MAG: 20
```

Better.

That evening, I tried something different in ID. Instead of wolves, I tried Cave.

[ID Create: Cave - Easy]

Dark, damp. Three rats, but bigger.

I killed them, but also practiced what Lyra said. Breathing. Feeling.

I sat after the fight, sword across my knees, and tried to feel my MP. 650 MP, just sitting there.

I closed my eyes. Breathed.

For a long time, nothing. Then, a faint warmth in my chest, near my heart. The books from enchanting melody had said: magic was absorbed by the heart, or at least near the heart on a metaphysical plane, and spread out through the body using metaphysical channels.

I focused on that warmth. Tried to move it to my hand.

A flicker. A tiny blue spark.

I opened my eyes. My hand was glowing faintly blue for half a second, then gone.

```
Skill Unlocked!
[Mana Control LVL 1/100]
Description: Ability to consciously manipulate mana.
```

And a new quest complete chime.

```
Quest Objective: Unlock your Mana - Complete! (Early)
Bonus EXP: 500
```

Holy shit. I did it.

I escaped ID, grinning like an idiot. I needed to show Lyra. But first, sleep. Two hours, full restore.

Tomorrow, I would buy that manual anyway. Knowing the theory would help.

And I still owed two silver.

One step at a time. Pragmatic. Grounded. Earned.
