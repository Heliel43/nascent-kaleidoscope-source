# Chapter 9: The Mine

Morning arrived, and as agreed, Lyra and I headed to the Adventurer's Guild. For a group that relied so much on paperwork and procedures, they were surprisingly understanding about the circumstances. We received our briefing, plus a commendation for our willingness to take the job.

The person we were talking to, the head of the Lornswatch Adventurer's Guild going by the name of Mira, turned an interesting shade of puce when we asked about loot rights.

"You get standard loot rights. 70% to guild for dungeon core, 30% to you plus all monster drops. Don't touch the core without gloves. And don't die. I have enough paperwork."

The iron mine was an hour south, an old scar in a hill. Wooden supports, rusted cart tracks. The entrance breathed cold air.

"This place was an iron mine until ten years ago, then it ran dry and they closed it. Dungeons form in abandoned places like this - ambient mana collects, monsters move in," Lyra explained, bow ready. "Scouts reported goblins and maybe a slime. Should be easy, but dungeons are never easy."

We entered, mana light in my left hand, iron sword in right. Thanks to Ambidextrous, both felt natural.

The mine was dark, but Mana Light helped. Level 1, dim, but enough to see tracks.

```
[Dungeon: Abandoned Iron Mine - E Rank]
Floors: 1 (Estimated)
Mobs: Goblin, Slime, Iron Beetle
Boss: Unknown
Status: Unstable
```

Observe told me that. Level 7 Observe now, more detailed.

First floor: goblins, three, Level 4. We killed them efficiently - Lyra arrow, me sword, no screams this time.

```
You have killed Goblin x3! 90 EXP!
```

Deeper, slime. Level 3, but resistant to physical.

```
[Iron Slime]
Level: 3
HP: 60
Weakness: Magic, Fire
Resistance: Physical 50%
```

My sword did half damage. Lyra's arrows did half.

"Use mana," she said.

I pushed mana into sword - faint blue glow - and slashed. Better.

-18 HP instead of -9.

We killed it, got slime core worth 5 copper.

Further in, the mine opened into a larger cavern. Old mining equipment, broken. And in center, a pulsing crystal, size of a head, embedded in rock.

"Dungeon core?" I whispered.

"No. That's too small. That's a mana vein. The core is deeper."

But around the vein, something moved. Iron Beetles, four, each size of dog, shells like iron.

```
[Iron Beetle]
Level: 6
HP: 100
Weakness: Joints, Underside
```

Tough.

Lyra shot one in joint, it screeched, flipped. I stabbed underside.

We fought, kiting, using aura. Lyra's wind aura made her faster, mine Iron Skin reduced damage when a beetle rammed me.

-30 HP, reduced to -25 by Iron Skin. Manageable.

After ten minutes, four beetles dead.

```
Level Up! Level 10!
5 Stat Points! 
Skill Level Up! [Iron Skin LVL1 -> LVL2] - 7% reduction
```

Level 10. Milestone. In many gamer fics, Level 10 gives class evolution.

```
Quest Alert!
Class Evolution Available!
You have reached Level 10 as Provisional Swordsman. Choose evolution:
[Swordsman - Standard] - Balanced, +5 STR, +5 DEX
[Spellblade - Rare] - Requires Mana Control LVL5+, +3 STR, +5 MAG, +5 INT, Unlocks [Mana Edge]
[Scout - Standard] - +5 DEX, +5 WIS, +3 LUK
```

Spellblade. That was what I wanted. Magic + sword, pragmatic, not pure mage.

I chose Spellblade.

```
Class Changed! Swordsman -> Spellblade!
Stats: +3 STR, +5 MAG, +5 INT
New Skill: [Mana Edge LVL1] - Coat blade in mana for +10 damage, costs 15 MP per 30 sec
Perk: [Adaptive Learning] - Learn weapon skills 20% faster
```

I felt the change - mana channels widening, as described in enchanting melody theory.

Lyra noticed. "You evolved? Already?"

"Spellblade."

"Rare class. Good. That'll help with boss."

Boss was in next cavern. We could hear it - wet, slithering.

We entered. The core room. A large crystal, size of torso, pulsing, and around it, a slime, but big. Very big.

```
[Slime Queen - Dungeon Boss]
Level: 12
HP: 500
Description: Mother of slimes, spawns smaller slimes. Weak to fire and piercing.
```

Level 12, 500 HP. Two of us, Level 10 and 12.

"We can do it," Lyra said. "Focus core? No, queen first. If we kill queen, core destabilizes and we can harvest."

Plan: I tank, she DPS.

I activated Mana Edge - sword glowed blue, brighter than before - and Iron Skin, and charged.

Queen spat acid slime.

-40 HP! HP: 960/1100

I slashed, Mana Edge cutting deeper.

-45 HP to queen!

Lyra shot, arrow with wind aura piercing.

-38 HP!

Queen spawned two small slimes, Level 3.

"Adds!" Lyra shouted, switching to knife, killing one quickly.

I killed other, then back to queen.

We fought, kiting around core. My MP dropping fast - Mana Edge 15 MP per 30 sec, Iron Skin 20 MP, Mana Light 5 MP. 1100 MP? Actually after evolution, MP was 800? Let's check: MAG 28 now, INT 30. MP should be 100 + MAG*15 + INT*10 = 100+420+300=820. Plus level. So maybe 900. I had about 900 MP, dropping.

Queen at 200 HP, enraged, spawned four slimes at once.

"Shit!"

Lyra was surrounded. One slime hit her.

-30 HP to Lyra!

I used last MP to cast Mana Light bright, blinding slimes, then slashed queen's core - the glowing nucleus inside slime.

Critical! -120 HP!

Queen shuddered, dissolved into blue motes and a large slime core.

```
You have killed Slime Queen LVL12! 300 EXP! Party Bonus!
Loot: [Large Slime Core - Worth 2 Silver] [Dungeon Core Fragment - E Rank] [Slime Queen Essence]
Level Up! Level 11!
```

We collapsed, breathing hard. Lyra's HP at 180/340, mine at 600/1100.

"Good job," she said, grinning despite blood.

"Good job."

We harvested. Dungeon core fragment - guild gets 70% but we get 30% value. Large slime core was ours.

Back at guild, Mira's eyes widened.

"You cleared it? E-rank mine, boss Level 12, with two people?"

"Yes."

She checked our cards, saw Level 11 and 12.

"Promotion points: 50 each. Need 100 for D-rank. You're halfway. Pay: 5 silver each as promised, plus loot."

We got: 5 silver (500 copper) each + 2 silver for large slime core + 30% of fragment (fragment worth 10 silver, 30% = 3 silver) = 10 silver total? Split 60/40: Lyra 6 silver, me 4 silver.

```
Money: 115 Copper + 400 Copper = 515 Copper (5 Silver 15 Copper)
Debt to Lyra: 100 Copper -> Paid! Remaining Money: 415 Copper
```

Debt cleared! Plus extra.

```
Quest Complete! Pay off debt!
Reward: 1000 EXP
Level Up! Level 12!
```

Level 12, same as Lyra now.

That night, tavern celebrated us. Rowan gave free stew.

Lyra sat next to me, not across, for first time.

"You know," she said quietly, so others wouldn't hear, "most partners I had left after first dungeon. Said I was too serious."

"Most partners I had died," I said, then winced. "Sorry, too dark."

She laughed, real laugh. "Pragmatic. I like it."

```
Affection: 25/100 (Friendly - Trust Building)
```

She leaned a little, shoulder touching mine.

"Tomorrow, no jobs. Rest day. You should... you should come with me to the lake. I need to wash gear, and you need to learn to not smell like slime."

"Is that a date?"

"Is that what you want it to be?"

I considered. Slow-burn, mature, not anime trope.

"I want it to be two people who trust each other washing slime off."

She smiled. "Good answer. Lake, noon."

We saved.

[Game Saved!]

Arc 2 had truly begun.
