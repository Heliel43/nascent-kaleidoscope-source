# Chapter 8: The Empty Field

The next morning I woke up just like I had been since the hospital feeling great and ready to start my day.

Body level 1. I'm actually human again. No, Level 8. But the feeling was similar - after two hours of sleep, I was fully restored, no aches.

I had a routine now. Morning: real jobs. Afternoon: ID. Evening: theory.

Lyra was gone for two days on a courier job to the next town. She left me with a note: "Don't die. And don't take any jobs above E-rank while I'm gone. - L"

Pragmatic to the end.

So I had time to grind.

[ID Create: Forest - Medium]

The world shifted. This forest was denser than Easy. Five wolves, one alpha, all Level 4-5.

I drew my sword - not the rusted one anymore. I had bought a better one with my remaining 15 copper plus 20 copper loan from Rowan: [Iron Short Sword] Rank E, Damage 12-18, Durability 100/100. Not great, but not rusted.

First wolf lunged. I parried, using Ambidextrous perk I had unlocked last night after leveling Blades to 5.

```
Perk Unlocked!
[Ambidextrous]
Use either hand as competently as dominant hand.
```

I had been training blades a lot easier since I didn't have to worry about shooting holes in the wall with it. Letting me keep the XP grind coming. I was almost at level 6 Blades.

The wolf died. Second, third.

Alpha came. Level 6, 150 HP. Bigger, smarter. It circled, tried to flank. I used Mana Control - pushed MP to legs, dashed sideways, slashed its hamstring.

-42 HP! Critical!

It yelped, fell. I finished it.

```
You have killed Forest Wolf Alpha LVL6! 80 EXP!
Loot: [Alpha Fang] [Pelt x2]
Skill Level Up! [Short Sword Proficiency LVL 2 -> LVL 4]
```

I was breathing hard. SP at 300/650. I sat, meditated, felt mana.

The book from Mira said: magic was absorbed by the heart, or at least near the heart on a metaphysical plane, and spread out through the body using metaphysical channels for each spell. The better the channels, the more powerful the wizard. The channels start to stabilize around a child's eleventh birthday, which is why students are invited to Hogwarts at that stage - no, at that stage in Aelindra, why adventurers start training.

I tried to widen my channels. Breathe in, feel mana in air, pull to heart, push to hands.

Blue glow, stronger now. I could hold light for 10 seconds.

```
[Mana Control LVL 3 -> LVL 5]
MP Efficiency +10%
```

After an hour, I had cleared the ID. Rewards: 400 EXP, some pelts.

I escaped, sold pelts at market for 30 copper. Bought food.

Afternoon, second ID.

[ID Create: Cave - Medium]

Dark, rats but Level 5, plus a Cave Troll mini-boss Level 10.

I died the first time. Well, almost died - HP to 50, then escaped.

Second time, I kited. Used sword, used mana to make light so I could see, used Observe to find weak points.

```
[Cave Troll]
Level: 10
HP: 400
Weakness: Fire, Eyes
```

No fire yet. But eyes - I could do that.

I blinded it with light spell, then stabbed eyes. Messy, but worked.

```
You have killed Cave Troll LVL10! 200 EXP! First time bonus!
Loot: [Troll Blood x1] [Cave Mushroom x5]
Level Up! Level 9!
```

Troll blood was worth 50 copper to alchemist. Good.

That evening, I studied the manual. It had a chapter on Aura - similar to what Lyra had. Scout's aura makes faster, quieter. Swordsman's aura would make stronger, more resilient.

It said: "A student can slowly use these channels to widen them, allowing them to hold and pass through more magic, making them more powerful. However, on the seventeenth birthday, the magic stabilizes, and the channels stop growing or shrinking. Of course, there are probably a lot of dark rituals to circumvent these limitations."

I was 19 in this body, so my channels should be stable, but Gamer Body might bypass that. Adept trait said body can adapt to anything within reason.

I tried to form aura. Not wind like Lyra, but something solid. I pushed mana to skin, imagined armor.

Faint blue sheen for 2 seconds, then gone. MP -30.

Progress.

Lyra returned on third day, tired, dusty, but alive.

"You didn't die," she said, seeing me in tavern.

"You sound disappointed."

"Relieved, actually. Means I don't have to find new partner." She dropped a bag on table. "Courier job paid 3 silver. I have enough to pay off half my debt now."

"Congrats."

She looked at me. "You're Level 9 now. I can see it. Your movements are... less like a book."

"Thanks. I've been training in my... safe space."

"ID, you called it?"

"Yeah."

She nodded. "Good. Then you're ready for tomorrow. Guild has a real dungeon opening. Old iron mine, south. It's been dormant for years, but scouts report activity. E-rank team needed to investigate. Pay is 5 silver each, plus loot rights."

Dungeon. Real dungeon, not ID.

"How dangerous?"

"E-rank dungeon. Should be Level 5-10 mobs, boss maybe 12-15. With two of us, doable if we're careful. And if we clear it, we both get promotion points to D-rank."

D-rank. That was a big jump. D-rank adventurers could take jobs in cities, not just frontier towns.

"I'm in."

"Good. Meet at guild at dawn. Bring your better sword, bring bandages, bring that mana light you keep practicing. It'll be dark in there."

She left to sleep. I stayed, practicing mana light until MP ran out, then slept two hours, restored, practiced again.

```
[Mana Control LVL 5 -> LVL 7]
Skill Unlocked! [Mana Light LVL1]
Skill Unlocked! [Minor Aura - Iron Skin LVL1] - Reduces physical damage by 5% for 10 seconds, costs 20 MP
```

Iron Skin. Not great, but better than nothing.

I saved.

[Save? Y/N] Y

Tomorrow, real dungeon.
