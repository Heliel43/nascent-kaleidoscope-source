# Chapter 15: Old Fort

The old fort was south, half day from Lorn City, near the hills. It had been a watch fort ten years ago, then abandoned when iron mine ran dry. Now dungeon.

We took job: Old Fort - Goblin/Orc nest, D-rank, 20 silver each, plus loot. Party of three, need healer but we had potions.

We set out at dawn, same as before. Carts? No, just us.

The fort was visible from distance - stone walls, partially collapsed, wooden palisade around, overgrown. Like in Hobbit, Bilbo's generous offer, but fort not home.

"Fort was built to watch road," Brann said, dwarf knowledge. "Stone, timber, watchtower covered in - notice boards? No, arrow slits. Should be solid, but goblins took it."

We approached. Observe:

```
[Dungeon: Old Fort - D Rank]
Floors: 2 (Fort + Basement)
Mobs: Goblin, Hobgoblin, Orc, Orc Warrior
Boss: Orc Chieftain Level 18 (Estimated)
Status: Occupied
```

Level 18 boss, higher than us (Level 14). But party of three, Level 13-14, with ID training, doable.

We entered via broken gate. Courtyard, overgrown, old training posts.

Goblins, four, Level 6-7, on guard.

Plan same: Lyra shoots one, Brann charges second, I flank third, fourth we kill before scream.

It worked, but one did scream, high ugly sound, alerting inside.

"Shit, reinforcements," Lyra said.

From fort interior, orcs emerged. Two orcs, Level 10, bigger than hobgoblin, green, tusks, crude armor.

```
[Orc]
Level: 10
HP: 200
Description: Strong, dumb, hits hard.
```

Brann tanked one, axe vs axe. I took other, Mana Edge + Iron Skin.

-40 HP to me, reduced to -36. My HP 1200 now at Level 14, so okay.

Lyra kited, shot.

We killed both, but took damage.

```
You have killed Orc x2! 200 EXP!
```

Inside fort, more goblins, plus hobgoblin Level 12, like before but with better armor.

We cleared floor 1 after hour, took potions (bought 2 small healing potions for 20 copper each, heals 100 HP).

```
HP: 900/1200 (Kael), 250/360 (Lyra), 400/500 (Brann)
```

Basement entrance, dark, cold, mana light needed.

Basement: old storage, now nest. Smell worse than sewers.

Boss room: Orc Chieftain.

```
[Orc Chieftain]
Level: 18
HP: 600
Description: Leader, smarter, stronger, has [War Cry] buffs allies.
```

Plus two orc warriors Level 12.

"Focus chieftain, ignore warriors, Brann tank warriors," Lyra said, party leader now.

We charged.

Brann activated Dwarven Iron Skin (15% reduc) + [Taunt] - new skill he learned, makes enemies focus him.

Warriors focused Brann.

I and Lyra focused chieftain.

Chieftain roared, War Cry, warriors got +10% damage.

-50 HP to Brann, reduced.

I slashed chieftain, Mana Edge Level 7 +25 damage, plus base 18 = 43 damage per hit.

-43 HP to chieftain!

Lyra shot, -35.

Chieftain hit me, -60 HP, reduced to -54 by Iron Skin.

We traded. At 300 HP, chieftain enraged, spawned goblin adds.

Brann was at 200 HP, still tanking.

I used last MP to cast Mana Light bright, blinding adds, then focused chieftain core - heart.

Critical! -90 HP!

Chieftain fell, dissolved.

```
You have killed Orc Chieftain LVL18! 400 EXP! Party Bonus!
Loot: [Orc Chieftain Axe - D Rank] [Dungeon Core Fragment - D Rank] [Gold x1]
Level Up! Level 15!
```

Warriors, seeing chieftain dead, tried to run, Brann killed one, Lyra other.

Silence.

We looted, found gold coin (100 silver = 10,000 copper! Huge), plus core fragment worth 20 silver, 30% = 6 silver each.

Back at guild, Selene's eyes widened.

"You cleared Old Fort? Chieftain Level 18, with three Level 14s?"

"Yes."

"Promotion points: 100 each. You are now C-rank eligible, but need test. For now, D-rank high. Pay: 20 silver each + loot."

```
Money: 2715 Copper + 2000 Copper + 600 Copper (core) + 10000 Copper (gold) = 15315 Copper (153 Silver 15 Copper)
```

We were rich.

Brann's debt: 25 left -> Paid! Plus extra.

Lyra's debt: 5 left -> Paid! Plus extra, farm safe.

Mine: 0, plus savings for house.

We celebrated, but also tired.

That night, Lyra said, "Farm safe now. Debt paid. Thank you."

"Thank you for trusting."

Brann said, "Clan forge, I can now buy anvil. 100 silver, I have 100 now. Can go back, but... I like party. Maybe stay longer, earn more, then go back with extra."

"Stay," Lyra said. "We need tank."

"Stay," I said.

He stayed.

```
Affection: Lyra 58/100 (Close - Partner - Almost Romantic)
Affection: Brann 40/100 (Friendly - Brother)
Party: Level 15, 13, 14 -> Average 14, D-rank high
```

Next: C-rank test, healer search, and maybe house.

But for now, rest.

We saved.
