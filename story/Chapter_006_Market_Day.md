# Chapter 6: Market Day

I was exploring the city's equivalent of a local market district. Being the main place of commerce in the city, all I could see around are shops, stalls and tents that offer goods and services. Wagons and caravans pulled horses or other creatures were also a common sight. And there were also plenty of people around, comparable to a crowded bazaar.

Lornswatch's market was only twice a week, but it felt like the whole town came out. Farmers with root vegetables, a tinker with pots, a woman selling charms that Observe told me were fake.

I had 17 copper, a rusted sword, two wolf fangs, three rat tails, and a new skill I didn't fully understand.

I needed money. And I needed to learn how to not get ripped off.

I approached the general goods stall. The owner was a broad woman with arms like hams.

"How much for wolf fangs?" I asked, placing my two fangs on the counter.

She picked one up, examined it. "Damaged. Alpha?"

"Yes."

"Ten copper for both. They're chipped."

"They're from an alpha," I said, remembering a line from another story. "Alpha fangs have more mana density. Alchemists pay more."

She raised an eyebrow. "You an alchemist now?"

"No. But I have Observe. It says 'Mana-dense, useful for low-tier potions.' That's worth more than ten."

She snorted. "Observe, huh? Fancy. Fifteen."

"Twenty, and you throw in that whetstone."

She looked at the small whetstone on her stall, worth maybe 5 copper.

"Eighteen and the whetstone. Final."

"Deal."

```
You have gained 18 Copper!
Item Lost: Wolf Fang x2
Item Gained: Whetstone (Crude) - Rank F
Money: 35 Copper
```

Not great, but better. I was learning. In the real world, negotiation was like in Ghost_in_the_City - you needed to know the value of what you had and not be afraid to walk away.

Next stall: Rat tails. Less valuable.

"Rat tails, three, clean," I said.

"Five copper total. They're pest parts, kid."

"They're alchemical too. For antidotes. The healer buys them for six."

The stall owner, a thin man, sighed. "Six, then. But only because Rowan asked me to not rip you off too badly."

"Rowan?"

"He said you cleared his cellar. He appreciates it."

```
You have gained 6 Copper!
Money: 41 Copper
```

41 copper. Still far from 100.

I wandered. I used Observe on everything to grind it, but also to find opportunities.

```
[Sunpetal Bundle - 10 stalks]
Value: 20 copper
Owner: Lyra Quinn (on consignment)
```

Lyra was selling her herbs. She had left hers with Mira. Smart - guild took 10% but handled sale.

I found Lyra near the edge of the market, talking to a man in a better cloak - a merchant.

"No, Lorn, I told you, I can't take the northern route job. My team is dead and I have a trainee," she was saying, voice tight.

The man - Lorn, presumably the town's namesake family - shrugged. "Then you stay F-rank longer, Lyra. Debt doesn't wait. You know that."

"I know."

He walked away. Lyra saw me, her expression smoothing back to neutral.

"Market day," she said.

"Learned that," I said. "You have Sunpetal on consignment."

"Yeah. Need to pay off my own debt." She didn't elaborate.

We stood for a moment, watching the crowd. People were walking back and forth all around and for a moment I felt like… This was real. These were actual people, not just NPC's in a video game. I couldn't help myself but keep moving.

"How much debt do you have?" I asked quietly.

"Forty silver. Family farm. Failed harvest two years ago. Guild loan."

Forty silver. That was 4000 copper. My 3 silver debt felt tiny in comparison.

"That's... a lot."

"Pragmatic, remember? I work, I pay. Slowly." She looked at me. "You have Mana Control now, right? I saw the flicker yesterday when you were trying to meditate behind the tavern. You're terrible at hiding it."

"You saw that?"

"Scout. I see everything." She almost smiled. "Good. That means you can take E-rank jobs soon. Better pay."

"Guildmaster said I need to complete three F-rank quests first."

"You've done rats. That's one. Herb gathering with me was two, even if split. One more and you can test for E."

I nodded. "What should I take?"

"Ditch cleaning. It's shit work, literally, but it's 6 copper and counts. And Rowan will give you another rat job in a few days - they always come back."

Pragmatic advice. I took it.

The ditch cleaning was exactly as described. South road, drainage ditch clogged with mud and leaves. I spent two hours with a shovel, SP draining, END ticking up slowly.

```
+20 END EXP
Quest Complete! Ditch Cleaning!
Reward: 6 Copper
```

```
Money: 47 Copper
```

Still not enough for the mana manual, but I was getting there.

That evening, I went back to ID. Not for wolves. For practice.

[ID Create: Empty Field]

I sat, and tried to do what Lyra did. Feel mana, move it.

The thick book closes with a "whap!" - no, focus.

I held out my hand. Blue spark, then a little more. A ball of light, faint, like a candle.

```
[Mana Control LVL 1 -> LVL 2]
MP Cost: 5
```

I was using MP. 5 MP for a tiny light. Inefficient, but it was something.

I kept practicing until MP was at half, then escaped and slept two hours to restore.

When I woke, it was night. The tavern was quiet. Lyra was at a table, cleaning her bow, alone.

"You should sleep," she said without looking up.

"I did. Two hours. I'm good."

"Gamer Body, right? You mentioned it yesterday when you were half-asleep. Said you restore when you sleep."

I froze. I had mentioned it?

"Don't worry," she said, finally looking up. "I won't tell. But... I've heard stories. People with weird abilities. Some are blessed by gods. Some are cursed. Which are you?"

I considered. Honesty, but edited.

"Blessed, I think. I see numbers. I can train faster. I can make a... safe space to train. But it's not free. I still have to work."

She nodded slowly. "Blessed then. Just... be careful who you tell. The Church doesn't like blessings they didn't give."

"Noted."

She went back to her bow. After a moment, she said, "Tomorrow, I'm taking a job to clear a small goblin nest. E-rank. Two silver pay. If you can get to E-rank by then, you can come. Split 60/40. Your share would clear your debt."

Goblin nest. That sounded dangerous. Level?

"How many goblins?"

"Five, maybe six. Level 3-5 each. One hobgoblin, Level 8. That's why it's E-rank."

Level 8 hobgoblin. I was Level 7, with a rusted sword and Level 2 Mana Control.

"Can I get to E-rank by tomorrow?"

"If you finish your third F-rank tonight and pass the test in the morning, yes."

"What test?"

"Guild test. Spar with the Guildmaster's assistant. Don't lose too badly."

I looked at my stats. 47 copper, Level 7, short sword Level 2, mana control Level 2.

"I'll do it."

She studied me, then nodded. "Good. Don't die. I need that two silver."

Affection: 10/100 (Neutral - Curious)

It was progress.

I went to Mira that night, before closing.

"I need a third F-rank quest. Anything."

She looked at her board. "Laundry. For the barracks. 4 copper."

"I'll take it."

Laundry was even worse than ditch cleaning, but it was quiet, and I could practice mana control while scrubbing.

By midnight, I was done. Hands raw, but quest complete.

```
Quest Complete! Laundry!
Reward: 4 Copper
Money: 51 Copper
Quest Alert Objective Complete: 3 F-rank quests!
Return to Guildmaster for Rank Up!
```

I was ready.
