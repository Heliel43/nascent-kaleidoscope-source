# Chapter 7: Rank Up

The next morning I woke up just like I had been since the hospital feeling great and ready to start my day.

No hospital. Tavern. Straw pallet. But the feeling was the same - full HP, full MP, full SP, ready.

I went to the guild early. Mira was already there, wavy hair tied back, stamping cards.

"Kael Thorne. Three F-rank quests complete. You want rank up test?"

"Yes."

"Guildmaster's assistant is in the yard. Try not to die."

The yard was behind the guild, a small dirt ring with wooden posts. A man was there, maybe 30, lean, with two wooden swords.

"So you're the survivor," he said. "I'm Jax. E-rank, been here three years. Test is simple: last two minutes against me with wooden sword, or land one clean hit. Most F-ranks fail the first time. That's okay."

"Okay."

He tossed me a wooden sword. I caught it. Observe:

```
[Wooden Training Sword] - Rank F
Damage: 1-3 (non-lethal)
Durability: Infinite (training)
```

"Begin."

He came at me, fast but not full speed. I had seen this before - in Ghost_in_the_City, when Hiromi commented on knife work. "You are better with a gun than you were before. You were kinda hopeless, but you are worse with a Knife." The point was, I had Muscle Memory, but Jax had real experience.

I blocked, the wood clacking. My arms vibrated.

He feinted, I fell for it, he tapped my shoulder.

"One."

We reset.

Second exchange - I tried to use what I learned in ID. Wolves don't feint. Humans do. I watched his feet, not his sword. When he stepped left, I stepped right and swung.

He parried easily, but nodded. "Better."

We went on. I was sweating, SP dropping. 580 SP, down to 400 after a minute.

*100 Body Experience Gained.* No, *50 Sword EXP Gained.*

At 1:30, I saw an opening. He was getting bored, slowing down. I used the trick Lyra taught me - don't think about the sword, think about breathing. Mana Control was still low, but I could feel SP and MP as similar pools.

I pushed a tiny bit of MP into my legs, like Lyra's wind aura but cruder.

A faint blue flicker, and I moved faster than expected.

My wooden sword tapped his ribs.

"Hit," he said, stepping back, surprised. "Clean hit. You used mana?"

"Maybe. A little."

"Most F-ranks can't do that for a month. You said you just unlocked yesterday?"

"Yes."

He studied me. "Blessed, huh?"

"Something like that."

He shrugged. "Not my business. You pass. E-rank."

```
Quest Complete!
Quest: Welcome to Lornswatch!
Objective 1 (Complete): Complete 3 F-rank quests
Objective 2 (In Progress): Pay off debt (3 Silver) - 51/300 Copper
Objective 3 (Complete): Unlock your Mana
Rewards: 1500 EXP, 640 Credits (Converted: 64 Copper), Skill: [Mana Control LVL1] Already Unlocked, Rank Up to E
Level Up! Level 8!
```

```
[Adventurer Card Updated]
Name: Kael Thorne
Rank: E
Level: 8
Class: None -> Swordsman (Provisional)
Debt: 2 Silver (Lyra) + 1 Silver (Guild) - 51 Copper = 249 Copper remaining
Money: 51 + 64 = 115 Copper
New Skill: [Aura Sense LVL1] - Can sense mana around you
```

115 copper. More than 1 silver. I could buy the mana manual now.

I went inside. Mira stamped my card with a new seal, green instead of brown.

"E-rank. Congratulations. You are now considered a full-fledged adventurer by the guild and are allowed to take E-rank jobs. Don't die, please. Paperwork is annoying."

"Thanks."

Lyra was waiting by the board, already geared.

"Pass?" she asked.

"Pass."

"Good. Then let's go kill goblins."

We took the job from the board:

```
[Goblin Nest - East Trail - 5-6 Goblins + 1 Hobgoblin]
Rank: E
Pay: 2 Silver (200 Copper)
Party: Lyra Quinn (Scout) + Kael Thorne (Swordsman)
Split: 60/40
```

"Your share, 80 copper, will clear your guild debt and most of mine to you. After this, you owe me 20 copper instead of 200," Lyra said as we walked out of town, back toward the forest where I had died twice already.

"Generous."

"Pragmatic. I need a partner who isn't dead weight. If you can fight, I can take more jobs. More jobs, faster debt payoff."

"So I'm an investment."

"You're learning."

The east trail was quiet. Too quiet. The Ironbark trees seemed denser here, moss thicker.

"Goblins nest in old burrows. They like damp. The nest is near where Joren's team died, actually. Might be why they died - they stumbled on it."

Great.

We found it after an hour - a hole in a hillside, half-hidden by roots, with crude wooden spikes around it. Smell of rot and feces.

Lyra crouched, hand up. I crouched too.

"Three outside, on guard. Level 3 each. Hobgoblin inside, plus maybe two more. Plan: I shoot one, you charge second, we both kill third before it screams. Then we go inside."

"Okay."

She drew her bow, smooth. The arrow flew - silent, green-tinged with her aura - and took a goblin in the throat. It dissolved without a sound.

I was already moving. Rusted sword, now sharpened a little with my crude whetstone, MP flickering in my legs.

Second goblin turned, saw me, opened mouth to scream -

I didn't give it time. Stab, twist, like with the wolf. Not clean, but effective.

```
You have killed Goblin LVL3! 30 EXP!
```

Third goblin did scream, a high, ugly sound.

"Shit!" Lyra loosed again, but it ducked.

Then a roar from inside the burrow. Hobgoblin.

It emerged - bigger, maybe 7 feet, green skin, crude armor made of bones and leather, holding a cleaver that was almost as big as me.

```
[Hobgoblin]
Level: 8
HP: 250
Description: Goblin leader, stronger, smarter, still ugly.
```

Level 8. Same as me now, but with 250 HP vs my 1000? Actually I had 1000 HP at Level 8. Better.

But it had a cleaver.

It charged Lyra, ignoring me - saw her as bigger threat. Smart.

Lyra dodge rolled, but its cleaver clipped her leg.

-45 HP to Lyra!

"Lyra!"

"I'm fine! Hit it!"

I hit it. From behind, rusted sword into its back.

-18 HP. Not much. Its skin was tough.

It turned, backhanded me. I flew, hit a tree.

-78 HP!
HP: 922/1000

Okay, it hit hard.

Lyra was up, arrow nocked, shot its eye. It roared, clutching face.

I used the opening. Mana Control - I pushed MP into my sword arm, blue glow, and swung with both hands at its knee.

Critical! -65 HP!

It fell to one knee. Lyra was there, knife out, driving it into its throat with the same efficient move she used on the wolf.

It gurgled, then dissolved.

```
You have killed Hobgoblin LVL8! 150 EXP! Bonus EXP for party kill!
Loot: [Hobgoblin Cleaver - Damaged] [Goblin Ear x5] [Silver x1]
```

Two more goblins emerged from burrow, saw their leader dead, tried to run. Lyra shot one, I killed other.

Silence.

We were both breathing hard. Lyra's leg was bleeding, but not badly.

"You okay?" I asked.

"Fine. Just a scratch." She pulled out bandage, wrapped it quickly. "You? You took a hit."

"Gamer's Body. HP loss, not real damage. I'll be full in a few minutes."

She looked at me, then at the dissolving bodies.

"You're not terrible, for E-rank."

"High praise."

"Don't let it go to your head." But she was smiling, a little. Real smile this time.

```
Affection: 15/100 (Neutral - Respect)
```

We looted. Goblin ears were proof for guild - 5 ears = 10 copper each. Plus the silver coin from hobgoblin - 100 copper.

Back in town, Mira paid us: 200 copper + 50 copper for ears.

My share: 80 copper + 20 copper (my ears) = 100 copper.

```
Money: 115 + 100 = 215 Copper
Debt Paid: Guild 100 Copper (Cleared!), Lyra 100 Copper (Remaining: 100 Copper / 1 Silver)
Money After Debt: 115 Copper
```

I was still in debt to Lyra, but less. And I was E-rank.

That night, I bought the mana manual from Mira: "Basic Mana Theory for Adventurers - 100 Copper."

She handed me a thin book.

"This should keep you busy for a few weeks to digest everything," she said, echoing a line from another library.

I took it to my room. The book was basic, but useful. It talked about channels, about heart as buffer, about how magic was like a muscle. You could train your channels to grow as you grow up. Before the eleventh birthday, it could be dangerous, but after, slow training widened them.

It also had exercises. Breathing, meditation, pushing mana to limbs.

I practiced until midnight, then slept two hours, full restore.

Level 8. E-rank. Mana Control Level 3 now.

Arc 1 complete. I had survived, found a town, paid most debts, unlocked mana, and earned respect from a scout who didn't trust easily.

Tomorrow, Arc 2 would start. More training, ID grinding, maybe a real dungeon.

But for now, I slept.
