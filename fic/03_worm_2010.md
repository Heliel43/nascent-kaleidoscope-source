## Brockton Bay, as of the opening week
- **Empire Eighty-Eight:** the 800-foot anvil on every street in the German Quarter and half the schools. Kaiser + Purity (married 2008; separated 2009 — v), roughly twenty capes incl. Hookwolf, Crusader, the twins (Menja is *not* on the roster until she surfaces in the street-war era — do not put her in 2010 text), and an army of shop kids with lanyards; street membership in the hundreds; openly recruits at East End schools via "community cleanup" flyers. Blasto driven out of BB to Boston in April 2009 (v) — an E88 highlight reel everyone remembers.
- **ABB:** a warlord-museum of Lung's making — Oni Lee lobotomized and institutionalized years ago; Lung himself hasn't left the ABB tower's orbit in years and fights Armsmaster in *slow-motion ritual* (the 1990s–2000s fights; no Lung-vs-Armsmaster brawl scheduled in 2010, it happens in canon none — the *myth* of it matters more than the fact).
- **Merchants:** the Train Yard, Skidmark's junkie economy, tolerated like mold.
- **Coil:** not a word anyone street-level can say. His absence is load-bearing.
- **Protectorate ENE:** Armsmaster (late-model halberd, PR-strained), Miss Militia, **Battery** (Jenny — leads the Wards until Aegis's transfer, which is coming in December; college-bound by 2011), Assault (marrying Victoria Dallon spring 2011), Triumph (currently on loan to Boston — v), Velocity, Dauntless (v — mid-2010 transfer in; safe to keep), **Glory Girl** & New Wave operating openly on PR tours; **Gallant/Aegis still Boston** until the Happening. PRT building: ENE HQ; Wards dorm inside the Protectorate tower; the PRT's *school outreach* ("Wards recruitment booths") is a Winslow fixture Eli walks past.
- **Winslow High:** the glory hole exists; the bathrooms are the problem; two windows per bathroom stall; posters for a "Diversity Club" no one joined next to E88 "community cleanup" flyers; **the school starts September 14, 2010** (Tuesday) — ch 1 is day two. Sophia gone to San Diego with her record half-expunged; Emma & Madison (sophomores now) orbit each other like planets; Taylor Hebert is a freshman who eats lunch near a radiator and has a notebook she doesn't hide well.
- **City texture, Sept 2010:** post-Endbringer-alert whiplash (air-raid app notifications people mute), the Boat Graveyard's economy of stolen steel, the ferry to the mainland as the only "nice" thing in the East End, PHO boards (Eli reads ENE's for the alert system and the missing-persons megathreads — his internet habit, and a plot leak he'll regret), rent rising because of the ferry, the Bay General wing Diane works on smells like iron at the end of her shifts.

## Hard canon-law reminders for this fic
- Triggers from **extremity** — no one in BB "gets powers" voluntarily; Eli has none and can't manufacture one without becoming a monster (and he *has* had the thought; log it as one of the ugly thoughts he doesn't say aloud, ch 3, and *never* lets recur casually).
- The Wards: two years, stipend, housing, schooling — and **no walkaway before eighteen**; his fanon brain screams *concentration camp*; canon reality is closer to *boarding school with lawyers* — the gap is `04_fanon_map.md` #7 and pays off at the Arc 1→2 hinge.
- The **Manton Effect** is a shard-enforced reflex, not a law capes cite aloud; street rumor says "the PRT can't test you for that." Both things being true is why Eli's first *builds* are non-weaponized.
- No cape-sense on PRT scanners for what Eli is (no shard — *yet*? never? Do not answer. R15.)
