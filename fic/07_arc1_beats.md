# ARC 1 — "The First Aid Kit" · ch 1.0–1.15 · Sept 14 – Oct 26, 2010

Target 8–15k/chapter. POV: Eli, first person past. Entry-image → the week's problem → a system-or-shop beat → exit-hook. **Tone law (user): fun serial — consequences, not tragedy. No manufactured loss, no lingering self-pity, no melodrama tax.** Home world frozen during the visit (R11) — plan accordingly: nobody back home ever has to notice a Tuesday missing. Interludes: none yet (arc 2+).

- **1.0 "The First Aid Kit"** — Sept 14–15. Winslow day two: the freshman bathroom, the pill-case girl, forty seconds of wrong adult timing, one wrong call he fixes, no heroics. Card lands after dinner, mid-argument about the electric bill: `[ WEEKLY WINDOW OPEN — NAME A TARGET ]`. Monday-anchor starts here. One word in the notebook. (The power arrives like a tax.)
- **1.1 "Nobody Sees Anything"** — Establishing: Nora, Portside Hardware, Kowalski's scrap bin, the bike, PHO boards, E88 table by the bus loop. The Dumpster beat (cape skirmish; he runs for cover, pulls a driver down with him, then does the *right* small thing twice). First rules draft; the **Sokka card** read aloud and laughed at *by the card itself* (`PACKAGE: planning, swordsmanship (self-taught, adequate)...`) — the user-vetoed pick, in-story, doing its job. Hook: he opens a fresh page titled **EASY and WORTH IT**.
- **1.2 "The List on the Fridge"** — The selection chapter (the fic's thesis, per `05` rules): rejects Aqua (rule 1), rejects Megumin (rule 6 + age — *his* addition, stated once, never preached), rejects YGO duels with dread, doesn't test Worm locals (the hallway moment, R13's founding). Lands on the Konosuba card, reads `STATE: eligible. She has accepted nothing yet.` aloud at the cereal box. Ends: he writes the *ask* as a draft letter to nobody, because you can't draft this one.
- **1.3 "Ask Her To Fight You"** — Two days of prep (the poncho, the gloves — "for the batteries," a lie to himself in real time, the plan that is *all* misdirection), arming, entry. `190 m` — the east road at dusk, a cart track, the smell of a town that runs on monster parts and gossip. Ends *before* the gate: he stands there, counting, then goes in like he's returning a library book.
- **1.4 "Register, Please"** — Guild day: registration as *Squire* (the clerk writes *Retired?*; keep the joke once), the welcome that's just *indifference at a reasonable price* (the user's clause, literalized: it welcomes foreigners naturally — nobody asks anything of him, and that *is* the marvel). First meeting: she mistakes him for a guild auditor; he doesn't correct it for eleven whole minutes; Berosal bites his sleeve. The *ask* — botched, then accepted exactly as drafted in `06`, and she sets it for "after the hunt, next Tuesday" because her week is *already* scheduled, which is the funniest sentence he's ever been told. Hook: six weeks until the duel. What does he do with them? "Get good. And, apparently: get *employed*."
- **1.5 "Axel, Unhurried"** — The linger chapter (mini-arc clause, the user's "without rushing"): porter work (the coin gag), the toad problem (twice; both times he's useful, not brave — splints, hauling, the horse-collar), Wiz's shop (a lich who clocks the *smell* of his pack and prices accordingly, 10% off, "come back"), the church, Megumin's arrival-and-misunderstanding (never corrected; she becomes the weird aunt of the arc), and the *practice*: toad-gait drills in the marsh edge at dawn. One awe beat, no worship (`09` r6: how a town that *outsources violence to freelancers* looks, through a Brockton kid's eyes — he likes it *less* than he thought; it's *cleaner* than the PRT and that bothers him). Hook: the smith, the smoke order, and — out of nowhere — the *first accidental heal*: a stable-hand's split palm closed by reflex while he says "hold still—", and the sound the room makes.
- **1.6 "The Duel, With Witnesses"** — Beat-locked in `06`. Crowd; chant-counting; the dummy; the wall; the empty; the staff goes away *with the mud trick*; the pin at nine; `RESULT: defeat (accepted, public, voluntary)`. Transfer as hearsay mid-let-go. The guild master offers membership; he declines (temptation = final beat, no speech). Ends with the *rematch-dinner invitation*, which is what the town does with a loss: feeds it.
- **1.7 "Party at the Guild, Consequences at Home"** — Dinner (warm, loud, the bard's *version* already wrong in a way he doesn't correct); Megumin loudly declares a duel of her own against *the toad* (he's her plus-one, lol, hold the shot); Berosal accepts a truce (one pastry, both bites at once). He mouths the ritual at a traffic-light moment on the way out — the last beat before `LEAVE` is the coin purse going *back to its owner* at the gate (the system's one joke, no caption). Return: **Tuesday, 19:08**, same seat, the pot still warm on the stove. Diane: "You're taller." He: "…posture." (Keep this exchange exactly this dry.)
- **1.8 "Homework of the Crimson Demon"** — The integration chapter with a spine: the crater he *doesn't* make (three nights of choosing walls of the Boat Graveyard, none; he builds the habit of *not* using the day's one on nothing); the first real explosion (the disused culvert, October 6, three-count on a stopwatch, *heard*; the PHO thread by midnight, the Armsmaster report by Friday — *logistics*, no doom); the **ceiling beat** (his own budget fails — he heals a stranger's kitchen hand at the shop counter, too much, and sleeps through two classes and half a Saturday; lesson is arithmetic, not grief: "I got her notes, not her twenty years"); his aim is still *terrible* for a week (toad physics is not *human* physics, and the crowd was part of the trick — the arc's quiet honesty: *he got away with it* and now has to live inside that). Hook: the merchant at the shop door, polite, asking for "the kid who builds things" — *the label's wrong now, it's "the kid who blows things," and it's spreading.*
- **1.9 "The Train Yard Fire"** (two-chapter movement) — Setup chapter: an October night, the squib cars, the *wrong* call that is his whole competence: he goes in *before* the cape arrives because the kid is inside now; and the day's one explosion — never burned yet that week, saved like change — goes into the *car wall*, not the fire, because an arch-wizard's first rule is aim at the structure, never at the hazard. The opening he makes is the arc's thesis in a sentence. the crowd's phone camera gets *back*, hood, one bad day. Ends: someone says "tinker" — wrong twice now — and the smell of the smoke on him is a *medal he's not sure about.*
- **1.10 "Nobody's Hero"** — The bill arrives *comedically, not tragically*: Glory Girl (canon voice: fourteen feet, halo, *curfew*, a speech nobody hears because she's mid-PR-tour and it's 11 p.m.), the news van that gets *his back* too, the school's *assembly* where the vice principal thanks "a Winslow student" without naming him and the entire East End internet names him by morning; **zero deaths** in the fire (the one bad second *is* dodged this time — the fic's promise to the user, and the *Worm* twist: it dodges because of *one more adult who showed up*, the stable-hand, the porter union, the *town* muscle — competence is contagious and he gets a *credit line*, not a crown). Ends: the outreach flyer — *used* — and Diane's hand over his at the table: "You're not going to stop." Not a question. Ten seconds of honesty, then soup. (Closest the arc comes; one paragraph, cut early per `09` r7.)
- **1.11 "The Petition"** — Anticlimax chapter: the bathroom-lock petition, the outreach office (he walks in holding the kitchen-counter flyer; the woman is *nothing like the fic*, fanon #5 bites *soft*), the PHO noise (E88 friends-by-flyer: the *wrong label* follows him out of the fire and he *corrects it once, in person, to a man with a lanyard*, which is the bravest scene in the chapter and reads like filing a noise complaint). Coil-thread, light version: the shop's new "contractor rate" letter arrives, professionally worded, *flattering about the culvert*, and — *the only sinister note allowed* — "we also admire your restraint at the Train Yard." Ends: he doesn't reply; he *frames* the letter (shop policy: mail goes where Kowalski can see it — *protection by publicity*, an idea the village's dinner culture taught him).
- **1.12 "Contractor Rate"** — The temptation chapter, played as *interview comedy*: the coffee shop, the man whose watch is wrong, the *job* (real money; fixes the bill; the *bill* is still the bill). The interview turns on Eli asking what "exclusivity" costs *in people*; the smile doesn't move; "think about November, Mr. Carter." Eli pays for his own coffee out of the same wallet that held a purse of gold this week, and the *joke lands on the reader*: the system took it; *he still tipped.* Ends: outside, the first cold snap, the *smell of snow*, and his hands doing a heal-micro-check on a classmate's scraped knuckles in the stairwell, casual as breathing — *that's* who he is now, the power is just *more of it.*
- **1.13 "The Beat 'Em Up"** — The storm (Oct 22–23): windows down, water in the underpasses, no capes anywhere, a *normal Tuesday with the lights out*: one call at a time (shop storm-shutters, Mrs. K's sump built from *guild* lessons in "rigs that just work", the kid on the L-street bridge, the *bike towed through the flood with a waypoint he set against his own garage wall* — **the first time the arc uses teleport at home: three seconds, one kid, zero witnesses, the most Worm thing this fic does**). Ends ER parking lot, shift change, the ten minutes, the non-question. (Mirror of 1.7's return: home *is* the host world.)
- **1.14 "Interlude: Berosal"** — *The first interlude, moved here from arc 2's plan because a demon's POV is a joke, not a wound:* Berosal's account of "the duel, according to my mother, who heard it from the bard, who is a *liar*"; Yunyun's *honest* version at dinner (she got to nine; she is *furious*; she is *also* — the interlude's last three lines — *already planning the rematch she knows can't happen* and what she'll say *instead*; hold the word "instead" to the end and cut). One beat of truth in comedy: the world kept moving *toward* her after he left. Home-world frozen ≠ her-world frozen. (If the user nixes interludes, this scene becomes the 1.15 epilogue in her third-person-close; same beats, same cut.)
- **1.15 "Next Monday, 00:00"** — Coda + fuse: the window opens (Oct 26); the `STATUS` line on the *next* card is **not** another world — `WORM LOCAL: eligibility test — DECLINE [Y/N]?`; he picks `Y`, the box goes, no reply (R13: the *refusal* is the scene; the reader gets the goosebumps; he gets *homework*); the city's mood (the Happening *building* on TV as a police-union story — *weather*, `03`); the notebook's next page: the roster's arc-2 shortlist, in his hand (diegetic `05`); Nora's horse drawing in his bag; headlamp click. The arc ends on an *obligation*: the cooldown expires in twenty-eight days and he has *a list*, a *ceiling*, and a *label he didn't pick.*
