# THE FANON MAP — Eli's Worm-knowledge and how it fails him

**Source discipline:** Eli never read Worm. He absorbed *fandom*: fanfics (a LOT of them, the good kind and the smut), CYOA builds, GoH arguments, TVTropes at 2 a.m., and at least one terrible YouTube recap. So his "canon" is (a) right-ish, (b) wrong in ways that were never wrong in the source material, and (c) **contaminated by fic logic he half-believes is real** — including fic-versions of *this exact premise*. The story's engine: every belief below is a gun, and the rule is **no Chekhov idle**. Format per row: belief → truth-in-world → when it bites.

1. **"Worm starts June 2011; the locker is summer."** → Wrong (fandom publication date imprinted on the fiction). Canon-start is mid-winter 2011. **Bites: ch 15** — he sees a freshman carrying a janitor's key ring for a "detention" he knows nothing about, and his calendar math goes quiet and wrong. Arc 2's spine: his "I graduate before any of it starts" plan dies in January.
2. **"Simurgh is secretly warning us; Ziz-truther takes."** → He absorbed it from fic commentaries; he doesn't believe it; he *checks himself* for believing it. **Bites: ch 13** — an Endbringer alert, and he does something clever and pointless (positions himself near "a trophy spot") and is ashamed of it.
3. **"Tattletale is a master and can't fight; Lung is forever; Bitch only talks to dogs."** → 1 is roughly right for the PRT file era; 2 is right and terrifying; 3 is *false* (her whole kit). He overweights the first because fic said so. **Bites: arc 2** — an Undersiders alley moment where every "should" he knew is 15° off.
4. **"Coil has tinker drones you can spot if you look."** → Coil is blaster/master; the soldiers are clones (rumor at best for Eli). His "watching for drones" habit reads, to actually-observant people, like a kid who's *watching PRT-adjacent things*. **Bites: end of arc 2** — Coil's file on him opens with: "the Carter kid looks up when flyers pass."
5. **"The Wards are a cage; registration is conscription; the PRT puts supers in cells."** → half-true, badly framed by edgy fics. **Bites: arc 1 finale/arc 2** — he *almost* reports the Winslow parking-lot cape incident to avoid "the Ward pipeline," and a real PRT outreach woman tells him, tiredly, the exact opposite of what fic taught him to expect. No lesson monologue; just the data point sitting there.
6. **"Triggers can be manufactured (Cauldron does it)."** → True but *way* above his paygrade, and the reason he once thought "I could make one happen to myself." He has not forgiven himself for the thought. **Bites: never** — as a *decision*; as a fear, repeatedly. Do not let the system ever seem to encourage it. (One line, arc 4, interlude-adjacent, from someone worse than him.)
7. **"Trigger range math, secondary powers, shard personalities, 'the entities need this to work like the wiki says'."** → his model is 60% real, 30% fanon, 10% fanfic of fanfic. Every power he *copies* violates at least one line of his model. **Bites: 1.9** — the hardware rule surprises him; the reader has already guessed it; the gap between guessed and learned is the joke and the horror.
8. **"Endbringers don't hit the same coast twice in a row."** → pure pattern-hallucination. **Bites: May 15, 2011** — when he's in town. (Arc 2+ endgame pressure; do not soften.)
9. **"Nilbog was contained by the PRT; Ellisburg is fixed."** → dome, quarantine, permanent. His geography confidence (he plans a runner route "through Maine") is wrong in shape. **Bites: planning only** — he scraps the plan when a card makes him *read the news*, saving the reader a trip. (This one is for the rule that his knowledge sometimes fails *early* and cheaply so it can fail expensively later.)
10. **"All SI-fics go one of two ways: hero with the harem, or villain who wins."** → meta-belief; the reason he *refuses both shapes* at every fork, which is the most human thing about him. **Bites: every arc's quiet scene.** If Eli ever starts narrating like a power-fantasy, the chapter is wrong.
11. **"Winslow gets worse in canon, so nothing can be salvaged there."** → false; it's *his graduation year*. Half a dozen fixable things are fixable by a senior who files a petition. He does. It works. Nobody thanks him; the vice principal is just *embarrassed*. **Bites: ch 14** — small, real, anticlimactic win (the bathroom door repair order is signed; a lock on a stall that isn't the glory hole; Nora's reading lamp). These are the victories the fic counts.
12. **"Emma Barnes is a monster; Sophia Hess is worse; Taylor is the victim who becomes something else."** → true and incomplete in ways he can't see from outside (Emma's parents, Sophia's home, Taylor's own teeth). He treats three kids as characters-in-a-book for exactly as long as it takes one of them to talk to him. **Bites: arc 2, the locker's aftermath — Eli does the ambulance thing: he's the one who calls it in from the second-floor railing and then stands there, useless, while the adults carry a stretcher past him, and someone he hasn't spoken to in his life says, flatly, "Thanks."** (That is Taylor, once, before she can be anyone's anything.)
13. **"Cape fights have rules you can predict (the cape-canon of fic: 'never engage a master at range, always break line of sight to a blaster')."** → street-accurate enough to keep him alive; too clean, which is why he hesitates two seconds too long in a Merchant panic (ch 13) and an ordinary man with a tire iron does what no "rule" predicted. First-aid training handles it; the plan does not. **Bites: arc 1 close**, one beat, no lecture.

## What he knows right — and uses constantly (do not nerf)
Lung's escalation; Kaiser's *lawsuit* strategy and PR war; Purity's "speech at a school" thing happens to *a* school (which one, he can't remember — see #11's optimism: he doesn't know it's Winslow until it is, arc 2); the Merchants are junkies with numbers; the Wards stipend is real money; PRT vans run ENE with a cadence he's timed out of boredom; Coil is *out there* and out of everyone's reach; Bakuda (a fanon name in his mouth — he doesn't know her yet; she's 2011); the Simurgh's Madison trophies are on video everywhere; *the Endbringers can be hurt* (the fact he clings to hardest, because it's the only one that promises a future).

## Handling rules for chapter drafting
- Never let Eli narrate "canon happens" as plot — canon is weather for him; the fic's plot is what his presence does to the weather.
- Every fanon belief appears *once* as a confident thought before it fails; failures get a *cost*, not a wink.
- When the reader could know it's wrong, let them know early (that dread is the genre); when Eli mustn't know, don't let the chapter wink.
