# ROADMAP — the shape of the whole fic (v1, replaceable)

**Structural law (learned from the bank's best):** *every* arc must be a Worm arc first and a power-fantasy second; the challenge world is the *engine* of change, the Bay is where the change *costs*. If a destination arc ever solves more of Eli than Eli's Bay week costs, the destination arc is overlong — cut it back.

## Arc 1 — The First Aid Kit (written, `07_arc1_beats.md`)
Sep 15 – Nov 8, 2010. The system's rules; the Mei win; craft-as-power; first visibility. Ends with the Happening approaching.

## Arc 2 — What The City Does With That (Nov 10 – Jan, 2011, "The Happening" arc)
The Happening from the street level (Eli: no cape fight to run *toward*, a triage to be *in*, and one decision — does he *use* a winch in front of a PRT camera?). Coil's contractor pressure gets a *second* lever (the shop's supply chain is "friendly" now, and it's the Merchants' territory, and Skidmark doesn't understand the word *rate*); the E88 wrong-label follows him into a "community dinner" invite Diane *accepts* before she learns whose table it is (the horror of that scene is her *poise*). Winslow closes on him: Sophia's empty desk, Emma's campaign, the freshman notebook, and *the locker in January* (fanon #1 detonates: he is home for winter break *inside the building* because of a make-up exam, *the* day; he calls it in; he is *not early*; he is *ninety seconds late*; that's the arc — the knowledge map failing exactly where he needed it exact). Second challenge lands in December **offscreen-adjacent** (the *dossier* is the arc-3 opener; the fight is told in one chapter *in medias res* — the reader gets the *costs*, the *world* is the next arc's treat — **and the target for #2 gets its full scoring + veto pass at arc 2's close, per `05_target_engine.md`: front-runner Mudman (MHA), whose win is only *possible* because the gas mask is an Eli build — the roster proving wins are earned, not drawn**). Arc close: the Wards' recruitment *boots* get a new name on a shortlist — his *own*, via the outreach woman who remembered the tourniquet kid. He says no in a way that sounds like *maybe*.

## Arc 3 — The Registration Squeeze (winter–spring 2011)
The PRT/Wards legal gravity (fanon #5's correction arc: it's *better* than the fic said and he must decide what that means, not that he was right); Lung recruits Bakuda **March 23, 2011** (canon sheet) and the bombing runs **early April 2011** — **Eli's first real "what can I actually do while it costs" set-piece: the bomb week** — three chapters of bomb-disposal *support* (a support-item engineer is a *resource* in that disaster — this is the fic's biggest "Worm arc first" promise; research pass required: her bomb types, the hospital, the wards' response; Eli does not *solve* it, he *keeps* people alive *between* solutions). The Lung/Oni-adjacent aftermath, the Simurgh *not* here (she's Canberra, Feb 2011 — the news-screen chapter), and the *cost* of being *seen by the right person*: Armsmaster's report includes "unregistered, cooperative, possible tinker-class *support* application" — a *classification* — and then the PRT does what the PRT does: a visit. Third challenge (arc-3's engine): **main character on purpose** (rule 6 broken in the story's thesis — the specific target only locks after the Armsmaster meeting draft, because the target must *answer* what the meeting *asks*).
*Guardrails for arc 3:* no new-buddy PRT, no instant recruitment, no "the PRT is evil" — the *law* is the antagonist (Wards Act, registration, the stipend with teeth). Eli's leverage is *being useful and not a cape*, and the arc's tension is how fast that stops being possible.

## The five promises (what the reader is buying; audit every chapter against these)
1. **Every power has a price and the price is *paid in chapter*, not narrated.**
2. **The city is a character that keeps its opinions** — Worm's problems don't bend to Eli; they *incorporate* him or eat him.
3. **The crossover is a *place*, not a loot box** — one world, one arc, one *door*.
4. **The system stays a machine** — the moment it winks, we've written the wrong fic (R15 enforced forever).
5. **Eli never stops being the guy who calls an ambulance** — the power changes the *radius*, never the *definition*.
