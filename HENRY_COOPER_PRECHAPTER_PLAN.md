# Henry Cooper — Pre-Chapter Production Plan

**Status:** final planning gate before Arc 1 chapter packets
**Purpose:** define how the story will be converted from bibles and arcs into chapters without skipping continuity or resource work.
**Current state:** no chapter prose exists.

This document is the last planning layer before chapter drafting. It does not write Chapter 1. It defines the scene-card system, research checks, opening movement, and approval gates that must be completed first.

---

## 1. Deliverables that now exist

The project has the following pre-chapter foundation:

1. `HENRY_COOPER_FOUNDATION.md` — first foundation, MCU skeleton, power rules, initial arc spine, and locked Thor/TVA correction.
2. `HENRY_COOPER_STORY_BIBLE.md` — premise, themes, tone, main/side-story architecture, canon protection, reveal and ending contracts.
3. `HENRY_COOPER_ARC_PLAN.md` — five books, sixteen arcs, main plots, side plots, side arcs, antagonist pressure, and interlude schedule.
4. `HENRY_COOPER_CHARACTER_BIBLES.md` — core/original/MCU character wants, needs, voices, knowledge, failures, and relationship end states.
5. `HENRY_COOPER_CONTINUITY_LEDGER.md` — dates, ages, records, TVA mechanics, evidence chain, powers, identities, injuries, and canon invariants.
6. `HENRY_COOPER_RESOURCE_LEDGER.md` — resource-by-resource mining and adaptation rules.

No chapter should be drafted from memory or from the foundation alone. The chapter packet must cite the relevant sections of all six layers.

## 2. Final pre-chapter decisions already locked

### Story

- start in fall 2014;
- Henry begins as a novice masked hero;
- Peter meets Henry after a dangerous mistake and teaches practical heroing;
- same school/class placement is a controlled chronology butterfly;
- AOU is the major parentage reveal era;
- *The Dark World* is not the reveal;
- Ultron remains the recognizable AOU antagonist;
- Thor/Henry pre-reveal is limited acquaintance only;
- Thor/Henry bond begins after the reveal;
- Loki is surprised, defensive, curious, and imperfect;
- Henry's response is mixed, not automatic hatred;
- TVA/Sylvie infant rescue is late author-side truth;
- no unexplained magical aging;
- Henry is Snapped with Peter for post-Endgame cohort continuity.

### Resource use

- use fitting source functions first;
- use source-combined structures second;
- preserve MCU character behavior;
- label provenance on planning cards;
- adapt, do not copy;
- exclude systems, gacha, harem, unrelated crossover cosmology, and generic power fantasy.

### Chapter scale

A 10,000-word average is a pacing target. A chapter may be shorter or longer when its movement is complete. No chapter is allowed to exist only to reach a word count.

## 5. Opening movement blueprint

This is not Chapter 1 prose. It is the approved sequence the Arc 1 packet must translate into scenes.

### Movement 1 — ordinary morning, wrong temperature

- Henry is in Elena's Queens home on a fall morning.
- He is doing something mundane: breakfast, washing a cup, or handling a school object.
- A small thermal mistake occurs: frost under his fingers, a frozen faucet, or condensation hardening on a surface.
- The first problem is cleanup and fear, not a special-effects announcement.
- Elena notices a practical irregularity but not the full power.

**Source functions:** `The_Spider` sudden power problem; `I just want to learn magic bro` mundane practicality; `enchanting melody` small effect before mastery.
**Knowledge:** Henry knows the event happened; Elena does not know why.
**Consequence:** Henry decides to test privately.

### Movement 2 — controlled test, uncontrolled cost

- Henry uses a safe object and a water source.
- The second attempt works better, then takes more out of him than expected.
- He learns that ice is not free and that temperature moves somewhere.
- He records observations in an ordinary notebook or phone note.
- He does not name an ability or decide he is destined.

**Power beat:** frost touch → small freeze → headache/thirst.
**Relationship beat:** Henry lies by omission to Elena.
**Clue:** no origin clue; the story protects the mystery.

### Movement 3 — first public choice

- Over the next several days, Henry notices a local danger that existing services are not reaching quickly enough.
- The danger must be concrete and local: a heat/pipe failure, a vehicle accident, a robbery with a civilian trapped, or a small fire where water/steam/ice interact.
- Henry chooses to intervene without enough training.
- He uses the wrong technique first, creating a secondary hazard.
- He saves at least one person, but the result is messy and visible.

**Source functions:** `The_Spider` first rescue and adult consequences; `Borne_of_Caution` environmental risk; `Yakuza` defense/terrain; `Kitsu` public/cape logistics.
**Rule:** the success must not make Henry competent overnight.

### Movement 4 — the bad mask and several days of mistakes

- Henry makes or acquires a provisional mask from ordinary materials.
- He tries small interventions over several nights/days.
- Each intervention teaches one different rule: dry air, fire/heat, civilian movement, falling ice, police arrival, or physical fatigue.
- Use compression and varied problems; do not write several interchangeable training scenes.
- His public identity acquires a provisional description from witnesses, but Henry does not choose an iconic name yet.

**Relationship beat:** Elena notices late nights and wet/damaged clothing.
**Power beat:** slide, brace, buckler, and slippery patch appear as practical solutions.
**Consequence:** a more dangerous situation finds him before he is ready.

### Movement 5 — Spider-Man intervention

- Henry attempts a rescue that involves height, speed, heat, or multiple civilians.
- Peter arrives because he is already patrolling, not because he has been tracking Henry's destiny.
- Peter's intervention is about movement and rescue order, not superior damage output.
- Henry is irritated by the correction because it is right.
- Peter helps him leave before the police and explains one or two rules.
- The boys do not become instant best friends or exchange full biographies in costume.

**Key rule:** Spider-Man saves Henry from his bad plan; he does not erase the consequences.
**Ending handoff:** the next school day puts Henry and Peter in the same class.

### Movement 6 — school collision

- Henry recognizes Peter through behavior, timing, injuries, and impossible excuses.
- Peter tries to act normal and fails.
- Their civilian conversation is about school first, not identities.
- Henry's suspicion remains a choice; he does not force a dramatic unmasking.
- The first Arc 2 pressure appears: they are now responsible for seeing each other every weekday.

**Source functions:** `The_Spider` school transition; `Dost_Thou_Even_Steal` beneath-mask trust; `A_Body_of_Water_and` social friction.
**Ending condition:** Henry has a peer connection and a more dangerous secret.

## 6. Arc 1 approval checklist

Before drafting any Arc 1 chapter packet, confirm:

### Character

- Henry is observant but not omniscient;
- Peter is experienced but still a teenager;
- Elena is caring and practical, not abusive or oblivious;
- Henry's orphanhood affects logistics but does not generate gratuitous suffering;
- the first masked period lasts several days/weeks, not one montage paragraph.

### Power

- moisture/heat/environment are visible;
- at least one technique fails;
- at least one construct breaks;
- a living target is not blood-frozen;
- Henry pays a physical cost;
- movement and rescue are more important than projectiles;
- the mask and gloves are imperfect.

### Mystery

- no Loki name in Henry's opening POV;
- no TVA name or agent;
- no magical-aging explanation;
- the first record clue is ordinary enough to dismiss;
- the audience can understand the clue later without needing an early exposition dump.

### Canon

- post-Winter Soldier public conditions are background only;
- Peter's earlier patrol period is implied/available but does not overwrite canon;
- Spider-Man is a young MCU-compatible hero;
- no Avengers cameo is needed in the opening.

### Source

- scene functions are labeled;
- no source-specific character or dialogue has been transplanted;
- the opening feels like Henry's MCU story after adaptation;
- practical QQ rhythm is present without copying a narrator.

- inspect school/social transitions in `Steal_the_Truth_Reach.txt` and `A_Body_of_Water_and.txt`;
- record any newly mined function in the resource ledger before using it.

### Continuity checks

- date, weather, school day, and age;
- Henry's available equipment and household resources;
- Peter's identity status;
- Elena's work schedule and knowledge;
- Henry's current power state and injuries;
- no early Thor closeness;
- no early TVA language.

## 8. Long-form chapter rules

1. A chapter needs an external problem, even if quiet.
2. A chapter needs at least one relationship movement.
3. A chapter needs one knowledge/continuity change.
4. A chapter needs a consequence.
5. A fight must have a physical layout and an exit problem.
6. A school chapter must not be a pause button; it changes social or investigative pressure.
7. Training must test a limitation or decision, not only display improvement.
8. Interludes must earn their placement and cannot reveal future truth early.
9. Do not end every chapter on a cliffhanger; a completed local movement can carry forward without a fake shock.
10. Do not solve a mystery because the chapter has reached its word target.
11. Do not increase scale merely because the previous chapter had a fight.
12. Do not make every adult either useless or secretly evil.
13. Do not make Henry the emotional center of a canon character's independent event.
14. If a scene exists only to include a favorite MCU cameo, cut it or convert it into a meaningful interlude.

---

## 9. Drafting order after approval

1. Finalize the exact Arc 1 date and opening weather.
2. Build Arc 1 chapter packets from the opening movement blueprint.
3. Build each packet's scene cards.
4. Run the continuity template for every packet.
5. Run the resource provenance card for every scene.
6. Draft Chapter 1 only after the first packet passes all checks.
7. After every drafted chapter, update the knowledge, injury, power, and consequence ledgers before drafting the next.
8. Do not draft Arc 2 merely because Arc 1 has a chapter count; audit the finished prose against its intended movements first.
