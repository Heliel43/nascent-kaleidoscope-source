# PHASE 7 — TRANSITION TO LUGUNICA (CH. 23, "HOW MANY TIMES") + POST-CROSSING POLICY

**Purpose (directive §§12, 22 Phase 7):** design how the end of the Yaldabaoth conflict — extended through the locked full-Royal third semester — results in Haru crossing into the Re world; fix the crossing's choreography, Haru's arrival inventory and state, the epilogue sting, and the post-crossing Earth policy that governs Book II's first chapters.

---

## 1. THE CAUSAL CHAIN (locked; formalized)

Yaldabaoth falls (Dec 24) → the distortion does not lift (canon) → Maruki's actualization takes hold (Jan 2) → Haru lives inside the world where Subaru never left; the anchor goes silent while the thread points elsewhere (ch. 20) → the Thieves reassemble; Haru's refusal is the banner (ch. 21) → the palace approach through wish-made-labyrinth (ch. 22) → calling card Feb 2, palace entered Feb 3, the final argument, **the reversion** (canon "Day of Fates") → **the unmaking of the counterfeit Subaru wounds reality precisely where Haru's whole self is anchored — the thread pulls, the tear catches the boy the god of control could never grip — and Haru is ejected across realities.** The crossing is therefore not an accident (pack v1) and not an escape: it is the *price of the choice he already made* (ch. 21) arriving one scene later than anyone could plan for.

**Design law:** the crossing must feel earned by character (he refused the perfect world) and prepared by mechanism (the anchor), while remaining un-foreseeable to him. He does not choose to cross. He chose truth; the truth had a fare.

## 2. CH. 23 BEAT MAP (Jan 29 – Feb 3)

1. **The eve:** the team's last quiet page (ch. 22's navigation chapter ends at the threshold; ch. 23 opens on the vigil — curry at Leblanc, gear checks, Makoto's checklist, Okumura Haru and Haru splitting the watch). Comedy ≤10%; the treaty's wordless version.
2. **The calling card (Feb 2, canon):** sent. The city that took the deal sleeps on it. Haru's page: the false Subaru's dinner from ch. 20–21 recalled in one paragraph — the thing he is about to unmake wearing his mother's table.
3. **The descent (Feb 3):** the palace's last stretch — the labyrinth Haru has learned to read (ch. 22's hinge pays off in craft: route-calls that save minutes and one bad fall).
4. **Maruki's final argument (the debate — Faith's register):** the offer named in full, to Haru personally, by the man who means it kindly: *you've lived there. You had him back. You had a world with no empty room. You would take that away — from your mother, from yourself — for a truth that only hurts?* And the Thieves' canon answers braided in. **Haru's answer is ch. 21's already-made choice restated without new arguments** (the book does not re-litigate; the exam was passed a chapter ago): the raw line here is the mirror line, held for the ending.
5. **The battle-that-is-a-debate:** Maruki's Adam Kadmon — canon fight, Thieves' roles; Haru the fist, human-scale, refusing glory. **No evolution** (all four Theseus triggers unmet — he is still, correctly, Don Quixote; the pack's Book II earns it).
6. **The consent (canon beat, Haru-shaped):** Maruki's reality requires acceptance to stand; the fight ends when the counselor accepts his own answer. The city's wish begins to unwind.
7. **THE REVERSION (the crossing's staging):** the false world unwrites from the edges inward. Show it at ground level first — the breakfast table's morning light going to gray, the false Subaru mid-sentence at the stove (if Haru is present for the family's unwind: **he is**; the book owes the counterfeit a witnessed end — "a very good drawing" erased gently). The unmaking reaches the brother-shaped wound — and the anchor answers: **one pull, not a pain** — the first physical event on the thread since Dec 25, 2015 that is not agony. The thread has never pulled before. It pulls now.
8. **THE CROSSING:** the tear where the counterfeit was unmade is a seam in the room, and the seam is a door because the boy in front of it is already threaded elsewhere. No light show, no announcement: the world goes to paper at the edges, the way a Palace does when it comes down — and Haru falls through the page. **Sensory law:** cold, ink, the smell of the notebook's paper, Subaru's name in his own handwriting somewhere behind his eyes. Duration: a heartbeat. He does not get to say anything to anyone. (The not-saying is the wound the epilogue bleeds from.)
9. **Last Earth line (the mirror, inverted):** ch. 1 ended *"I'm coming, Subaru. One way or another."* Ch. 23's last Earth line is its answer arriving true — wording to prose, e.g. the narration completing the promise from the other side of it. **This is the chapter's single raw line.**
10. **THE STING (final call made: IN, as the book's last page):** one page, Thieves-side, post-reversion — the real world restored, the Leblanc corner table, the copied log (ch. 22's protocol paying off) sitting where he sat. Futaba reads the first page. Says the locked line: *"He's not dead. He's LOST. We find him."* — his own arc, returned with the polarity reversed. Book One ends mid-determination. **The Lugunica chapters do not begin until Book II.**

## 3. ARRIVAL STATE — WHAT HITS LUGUNICA (Book II entry conditions; Phase 8 consumes this)

- **Inventory:** winter jacket; the notebook (two years of pains log, training log, castles tab — the full evidentiary spine of the eventual reveal); **the folded verdict paper, worn soft at the fold, over his heart** (it will matter in Book II more than any weapon); phone (works as a phone, no network; the Nav aboard — its fate in a no-Metaverse world: open, Phase 9); street gear; Shizu's karaoke voucher (unused; comedy artifact); no money, no map, no plan that survives daylight.
- **Body:** fifteen-going-sixteen, mortal, tournament-tough, months of Persona-war conditioning — **the strongest ordinary boy in any world he's ever visited, and the weakest person in most rooms he's about to enter.** Wounds: none fresh. Sleep debt: real.
- **Persona:** DON QUIXOTE, fully grown — charge, guard, Rocinante's lend, **the sails (once-promoted at the god)** — resolve-scaled, Curse-weak, acceptance-born. No Theseus (triggers: FOUND unmet... until it isn't).
- **Knowledge:** Metaverse fluency; zero Re knowledge (locked); the pains' true cause unknown; the log dense, the thread pulled once.
- **Mind:** the delusion now *metaphysical and true in one respect* ("somewhere there's a castle with Subaru in it" — ch. 19) — arriving in the one world where that sentence is literally correct is the Book II engine.

## 4. POST-CROSSING EARTH POLICY (governs Book II's first chapters)

1. **Earth-side threads freeze, they do not resolve.** No Book II chapter may resolve the parents, the team's search, Iwai, the case files, or the Natsuki household. They exist in suspension — the pack default (reactions/epilogue material) — and are Book V/epilogue assets.
2. **The one open Earth valve:** the copied log (Futaba's copy). If and only if Phase 9 builds a communication mechanism (the Akechi module option (a) or a Velvet Room exception), it becomes a channel; default is silence.
3. **No rescue arc:** the Thieves cannot reach Lugunica. The sting's determination is emotional truth, not plot promise. (Directive: Lugunica is Haru's new stage; Earth's grief stays Earth's.)
4. **The anchor continues to work cross-world** (it pulled, once). Book II may use post-crossing pain echoes — Phase 8's to schedule (proposal: silence during Arc 1-era, resumption tied to Arc 2's loops, date-matchable by the reader, never by Haru).
5. **Everything Haru carries is on his body.** No lost-luggage conveniences; the notebook's survival is total (it is the reveal's spine).
