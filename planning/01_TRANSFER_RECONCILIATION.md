## 2. THE DEATH ANCHOR / PHANTOM PAINS — RECOMMENDATION: KEEP, RE-SOURCED (Q1)

The phantom-pain device is not Ajin physiology — it is the pack's engine, and it should survive with a new power source:

- **What it does (unchanged):** Haru feels death-echoes of Subaru's loops — pain without memory, **never information** (directive §16-compliant; it justifies multi-year refusal to accept death, the notebook prop, "gaps shrinking" escalation, and the foreshadow that pains arrive *before* the wound they echo).
- **What changes:** sourcing. "Natsuki blood" was fusion-invention shorthand; under the directive, supernatural ability must come from the established framework — i.e., **Persona metaphysics**. Proposal: the anchor is the cognitive shadow of an unusually large bond — the reason the Velvet Room takes interest in Haru at all; the reason Yaldabaoth's control-scheme *slipped* on him at the crossing. On Earth it is undiagnosable and quiet; in the Metaverse, Lavenza can see what it is and — per Velvet Room law — may only hint. (Rig the Game's cross-reality Velvet precedent, audit §2.5, supports "rules that bind what the Room may say.")
- **Payoff spine preserved:** the notebook log → evidence of a pattern no doctor can name → Book II's reveal where Haru finally *understands* what he's been feeling ("How many times, Subaru?") and speaks it so the Witch's taboo is never triggered. The device that started as the delusion's fuel ends as the proof of love. That is the strongest through-line in the old project and it survives the Ajin removal fully.

If the author prefers **cut** (Q1): the delusion must carry the motivation alone (it can — Steal-the-Truth-style refusal is sufficient), and the crossing re-sources to the Maruki-hole (Q2 option B) or the Yaldabaoth-doors image (audit §5.7). The log, the bath scene, and the reveal's evidentiary spine would be lost or rebuilt on weaker material.

## 3. P5 ROYAL INTEGRATION vs. "YALDABAOTH ENDS EARTH" (Q2) — THE ONE REAL STRUCTURAL CONFLICT

- **Pack (old default):** year follows *vanilla* P5 to Yaldabaoth, Dec 24, 2016; crossing happens mid-battle via the anchor misfire; third semester undecided.
- **Directive:** Rule 6 — P5R is a *major* pillar ("do not reduce…"); Rule 7 — Yaldabaoth ends Earth. In Royal's own storyline the third semester runs *after* the god falls (Jan–Feb, Maruki finale), so a naive merge breaks Rule 7.

**Three coherent resolutions (author's call):**

- **Option A — Vanilla year + Royal seasoning (old default, directive-minimum).** Yoshizawa and Maruki exist from their canon-Royal spring entrances; Maruki counsels Haru through the year (dangerous, given the anchor); no third semester. Crossing at Yaldabaoth exactly as the pack has it. *Cost:* the Maruki temptation — the single most personal beat available to a boy whose wish is "give my brother back" — is unused. Directive §9's warning applies.
- **Option B — Full Royal finale (RECOMMENDED).** Yaldabaoth falls Dec 24 and remains **Haru's final battle on Earth** (Rule 7 honored literally: no Thief-scale battle follows for him). The third semester begins: in Maruki's actualized reality, Haru gets exactly what he wants — *a world where Subaru never left* — and lives inside it long enough to feel the wrongness (the false Subaru is built from memory; he cannot surprise Haru; he does not feel like the brother, or — crueler — he "knows" things the anchor says Subaru never lived). The Thieves work to undo the reality from outside while Haru, inside, must choose the painful truth over the perfect lie — the Don Quixote delusion's final exam, taken from the *other side*: the delusion now wears Subaru's face. When reality reverts (~Feb 2), the wound torn where false-Subaru was unmade resonates with the death-anchor, and Haru is ejected across realities — **the crossing as the cost of refusing the fake brother.** Yaldabaoth = final battle; Maruki = final temptation; directive Rules 6+7 both honored; the pack's "accidental misfire" crossing is upgraded into the saga's darkest, most on-theme mechanism.
- **Option C — Compressed compromise.** Keep crossing at Dec 24; move a compressed Maruki confrontation into late December via artistic license. *Rejected as default:* it breaks pack hard rule #1 (no invented canon events) by reshuffling Royal's sequence.
