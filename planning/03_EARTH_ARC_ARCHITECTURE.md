## 2. CANON-MOMENTUM POLICY FOR BOOK ONE (directive §15, pack rule #1)

**Ruling: Book One changes ZERO canon outcomes.** The full P5R year runs its canon course; Haru exists inside it, is shaped by it, and shapes only texture, timing, and one trust dynamic. Rationale: (a) pack rule #1 is absolute; (b) the saga's real divergence is Haru himself — his induction into the team and, above all, **his disappearance at the crossing**, which is the next book's engine and the epilogue's wound; (c) directive §15's "events may still converge" is thematically load-bearing here — see the Okumura beat (ch. 13), where Haru's one serious attempt to change an outcome fails and the convergence becomes the chapter's meaning.

Touched-but-not-broken canon (the full map):
- **Kamoshida arc:** background witness only (pack rule #2; ch. 1's May 9 aftermath beat). No contact with the Thieves.
- **Madarame:** parallel only. Haru never enters their palace.
- **Kaneshiro:** Haru's independent entry nearly compromises the infiltration; the team's save preserves canon outcomes; the residue is *internal* (the team now knows a wildcard exists — affects trust texture, not events).
- **Futaba:** Haru is support during the pyramid; canon intact. Output: the notebook-analysis partnership (ch. 10).
- **Okumura:** Haru argues for verifying the collapse; canon holds (Okumura dies). Convergence as theme.
- **Sae/Shido:** Haru is deliberately locked out of the frame operation (his civilian identity can't survive the exposure) — his powerlessness during Ren's arrest is the year's lowest point, and canon requires the arrest anyway.
- **Yaldabaoth:** the anchor's slip is mechanism, not event change (locked; the god's scheme simply cannot find purchase on a boy already threaded elsewhere).
- **Third semester:** Haru accelerates the team's reassembly; outcome unchanged.
- **THE CROSSING:** the one true divergence. Everything after it on Earth is deferred material (pack default; Phase 7 rules on the sting's placement).

## 3. CORRECTIONS & AMENDMENTS TO PRIOR PLANS (found in canon verification)

1. **Kaneshiro collision moves to late June–July 9** (canon window Jun 19–Jul 9), not "June–July" loosely. The pack's "collides at Kaneshiro (June–July)" holds; the architecture pins it.
2. **Codename amendment — ROOK before KNIGHT.** The session-1 ruling placed the two-Harus collision at the Kaneshiro induction. Canon forbids it: Okumura Haru is not on the team until mid-September. Fix (spirit of the ruling preserved): at induction the team names him **ROOK** (the rookie piece; Morgana's opening bit). When Okumura Haru joins (ch. 12), the collision forces the crisis — both veto "Haru Two," both veto name-suffix splits in unison (the founding treaty), and Morgana lands **KNIGHT** ("he keeps throwing himself at castles"). The Persona double-meaning lands three months later than first planned and lands *harder* — on-page, the reader has watched him earn it.
3. **Third-semester dates pinned:** false reality begins ~Jan 2; Sumire rescue Jan 9; forced exploration Jan 12; calling card Feb 2; final battle and reversion **Feb 3 — canon's "Day of Fates."** The crossing is locked to the reversion, night of Feb 2–3, 2017. (The pack's old "deadline February third" memory is canon-exact — note the resonance for prose.)
4. **Mementos opens May 7, 2016** — the day ch. 1 begins. Use it: the Nav arriving days after the Metaverse "opens for business" is canon consonance the narration never comments on and the reader may catch.
5. **Maruki's hire is canon-motivated** (post-Kamoshida school response) — so his first Haru beat belongs in ch. 2's window, and the Oct 3 "first glimpse" of his palace (canon date) belongs in ch. 12's window. Both are planted quietly; neither is explained.
6. **"Kamoshida confession May 2" (pack) confirmed canon-exact** (deadline May 2). Ch. 1's May 9 "aftermath Monday" beat survives verification.
