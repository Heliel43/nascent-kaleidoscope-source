## 2. CANON-MOMENTUM POLICY FOR BOOK ONE (directive §15, pack rule #1)

**Ruling: Book One changes ZERO canon outcomes.** The P5R year runs its canon course; Haru exists inside it, is shaped by it, and shapes only texture, timing, and trust. Rationale: pack rule #1 is absolute; the saga's real divergence is Haru himself — his induction and, above all, **his disappearance at the crossing** (the next book's engine, the epilogue's wound). Directive §15's "events may still converge" is thematically load-bearing — paid at the Okumura collapse (ch. 14).

Touched-but-not-broken canon:
- **Kamoshida:** background witness only (pack rule #2; ch. 1 cold open, live).
- **Madarame:** parallel only (city noise; the Thieves' rising fame as background pressure on Haru's own war).
- **Kaneshiro:** Haru's earth-side investigation runs BESIDE the canon subplot (Makoto's hunt, the team's capture, Makoto's awakening — all offscreen/city-legend to him). On invasion day their operations collide; canon outcomes hold (calling card, confession). The residue is internal: the team now knows a wildcard exists.
- **Futaba:** support during the pyramid; canon intact. Output: the notebook-analysis partnership.
- **Okumura:** Haru argues for verifying the collapse; canon holds (convergence-as-theme).
- **Sae/Shido:** locked out of the frame op (his civilian identity can't survive exposure); his powerlessness during Ren's arrest is the year's lowest point.
- **Yaldabaoth:** the anchor-slip is mechanism, not event change.
- **Third semester:** Haru accelerates the team's reassembly; outcome unchanged.
- **THE CROSSING:** the one true divergence; post-crossing Earth is deferred material.

**Session-5 law (pre-awakening supernatural quarantine):** from ch. 1 to ch. 7, *nothing supernatural touches Haru* except the two established ch. 1 facts — the anchor pains and the Nav's "No results found." Persona characters (Ren, Ryuji, Ann, Morgana, Makoto, Mishima, Maruki, Akechi-on-TV) appear freely **as people**, in school/city/news texture, but the Metaverse stays closed to him until the Bank pulls him in. This is what makes chapters 2–6 a yakuza story, per the author's explicit instruction — and per directive §7's ladder (underworld first, supernatural later, Metaverse last).
