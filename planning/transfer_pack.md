Two days later Haru felt the first phantom pain: the Natsuki blood carries a "death anchor" (fusion invention) that lets Haru feel death-echoes of Subaru's Return-by-Death loops — feeling without memory, never intel. ~~Haru is also an Ajin (revive on death; natural healing otherwise; full pain).~~ [REMOVED — directive §14] Convinced his brother is alive and suffering, and unable to live with "he just vanished," Haru convinced himself the yakuza (Tojo Clan/Kamurocho) made Subaru disappear — a delusion built on mundane coincidences (maintenance-window camera glitch, a retired old man, a scared man's invented "Tojo pin"). This delusion is his Don Quixote windmill. By day he is an ordinary Shujin Academy first-year (family legacy + scholarship — NOT for investigation; school is just school). By night he is "Winter," a Kamurocho street brawler working his invented case. His Persona: Don Quixote (Fool) → Theseus (Sun) — invented Personas, not in the compendium; the evolution happens later when the delusion dies against the truth. A Meta-Nav app (canon: name/place/distortion fields, no talking) appears on his phone.

Earth arc = the full Persona 5 year, ~26 chapters × 10,000+ words each. The crossing happens during the final battle against Yaldabaoth (Dec 24, 2016), by accident: the god's world-fusing power misfires against Haru's death anchor and ejects him across realities to the Loot House in Lugunica (Book II onward). Futaba × Haru romance (wholesome, PG, blush-level; both 15, same school year). She is a canon shut-in until August — no early appearances.
