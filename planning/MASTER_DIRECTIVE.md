PROMPT 2 — HARU NATSUKI / RE FANFICTION MASTER DIRECTIVE

We are creating a long-form **Re** fanfiction centered around an original character:

**Haru Natsuki — Subaru Natsuki's younger brother.**

This is not a generic isekai story, not a generic OC story, and not a story where an original protagonist is inserted into Re simply to become overpowered.

The central premise is simple:

**Subaru Natsuki disappeared from Earth. Haru refuses to accept that his brother is gone and spends years searching for him.**

Everything else develops from that premise.

1. ABSOLUTE PRIORITY: USE THE PROVIDED SOURCE MATERIAL

Before developing the story, thoroughly inspect the **attached repository/source material**.

The repository contains substantial amounts of writing and previous creative work.

That material is the primary creative resource for this project.

Do NOT default to writing a new story from your own generic AI patterns.

This is extremely important.

I have deliberately provided a large body of existing human-written material because I want the resulting story to retain the specificity, personality, ideas, situations, character dynamics, and writing concepts already present in that material.

Your job is to **extract, understand, reorganize, combine, adapt, and connect the existing material into this fanfiction.**

Think of the repository as raw narrative material.

You are the story architect.

You are NOT the replacement author.

2. DO NOT INVENT A GENERIC AI STORY

Avoid the common AI tendency to produce:

- Generic dramatic monologues
- Generic "chosen one" plots
- Generic mysterious villains
- Generic inspirational speeches
- Generic fantasy dialogue
- Generic emotional scenes
- Generic power escalation
- Generic friendship speeches
- Generic descriptions that could belong to any fanfiction
- Excessive grandiosity
- Empty philosophical dialogue
- Characters behaving differently simply because the plot needs them to

Whenever an existing piece of source material can provide the foundation for a scene, character dynamic, conflict, joke, interaction, or plot development, **use that material instead of inventing something from scratch.**

You are allowed to create new connective material.

You are encouraged to create new connective material when necessary.

But new material should primarily serve to connect or adapt existing material.

The ratio should strongly favor:

**Existing human-written material → reorganized/adapted into a new story**

rather than:

**AI invents entirely new story → occasionally borrows an existing idea.**

3. REASSEMBLY IS ENCOURAGED

The source material does not need to be used in its original order.

You may:

- Rearrange scenes
- Combine scenes
- Split scenes
- Merge concepts
- Move characters between situations
- Recontextualize existing events
- Repurpose existing dialogue concepts
- Rebuild character arcs
- Combine separate plotlines
- Extract individual scenes from larger stories
- Use one source for a character dynamic and another for a plot structure
- Adapt an existing scene into a Persona or Re context
- Create new transitions between human-written sections
- Use old material in an entirely different chronological position

The goal is not to reproduce the source stories.

The goal is to discover **what story can be constructed from them.**

Do not assume every source element belongs in the final story.

Some material will be useful.

Some will be redundant.

Some will conflict.

Some may only provide a character trait, scene structure, joke, emotional beat, or conceptual framework.

Use judgment.

4. HUMAN WRITING SHOULD REMAIN HUMAN

Do not automatically write polished AI prose for every scene.

The final prose should eventually be produced by the human author.

Your job is primarily to determine:

- What happens
- Who is involved
- Why it happens
- What information each person possesses
- What each person believes
- What changes afterward
- How it connects to previous material
- How it affects future events
- Where existing source material can be incorporated
- Where new writing is actually necessary

When planning scenes, prefer detailed **story architecture and writing instructions** over automatically generating the final scene.

For example:

Scene Concept

Haru confronts someone connected to Subaru's disappearance.

Source Material Used

- Existing confrontation from Source A
- Character interaction from Source B
- Emotional dynamic from Source C

Adaptation

- Replace original setting with Kamurocho
- Preserve the original relationship tension
- Reframe the conflict around Subaru's disappearance
- Move the revelation later so Haru does not learn everything immediately

Scene Objective

Establish that Subaru's disappearance may be connected to something supernatural.

Human Writing

The actual prose and dialogue will be written later.

This is the kind of assistance I want.

5. YOU MAY REVIEW THE OTHER PROJECT'S HISTORY

The previous project we worked on contains relevant planning, character concepts, structural ideas, writing approaches, and other information that may help you understand the intended direction of this story.

When accessible, inspect the relevant previous-project material.

Do not blindly copy concepts from that project.

Instead, use it to understand:

- How the characters were conceived
- What narrative structures were previously discussed
- Which ideas were intentionally established
- What concepts were rejected
- What character dynamics were considered important
- What the intended tone and progression were

The goal is continuity of planning, not automatic continuity of every old idea.

6. HARU'S CORE PREMISE

Haru is:

**Subaru's younger brother.**

He is fully native to Earth.

He was born there.

He grew up there.

He has no reincarnated knowledge.

He has no meta knowledge.

He does not know Re.

He does not know Lugunica exists.

He does not know Subaru was transported to another world.

He does not know the future.

He does not know canon.

He does not possess "isekai protagonist knowledge."

This is critical.

Haru experiences the story naturally from his own perspective.

The audience may know Re.

Haru does not.

7. THE SEARCH FOR SUBARU

Subaru's disappearance is the initial engine of the story.

Haru does not simply accept:

"Subaru vanished, therefore he must have been isekai'd."

There is no reason for him to know that.

From Haru's perspective, his brother has simply disappeared under impossible circumstances.

His investigation should therefore develop naturally.

He follows leads.

He encounters people.

He enters dangerous environments.

He discovers increasingly strange phenomena.

He gets involved with the criminal underworld.

He eventually encounters the supernatural.

He eventually reaches the Metaverse.

And only much later does he discover that the world is much larger and stranger than he ever imagined.

8. EARTH ARC IS NOT A PROLOGUE

This is one of the most important structural instructions.

**The Earth Arc is LONG.**

Do not compress Earth's story into two or three chapters.

Do not treat it as a prologue that exists only to explain why Haru enters Lugunica.

The Earth Arc is the **first major saga of the story.**

It needs enough room for:

- Haru's relationship with Subaru to be established
- Subaru's disappearance to have emotional weight
- Haru's investigation to develop
- His personality to evolve
- His combat style to develop
- His involvement in Kamurocho to become meaningful
- His relationships with Earth characters to develop
- His supernatural experiences to escalate
- The Persona storyline to develop
- His Persona to evolve psychologically
- The Persona 5 Royal storyline to play out
- Haru to become a substantially different person by the end of the arc

The Earth Arc should feel like a complete story on its own.

Then Lugunica becomes the beginning of the next enormous stage of his life.

9. PERSONA 5 ROYAL IS A MAJOR PART OF THE EARTH ARC

The Earth Arc incorporates the story and major progression of **Persona 5 Royal**.

Do not reduce Persona 5 Royal to:

"Haru joins the Phantom Thieves and then the game ends."

The Persona storyline should be substantial.

Use the available source material to determine how Haru fits into the existing Persona framework.

The Persona 5 Royal elements should be integrated into Haru's existing search for Subaru rather than existing as an unrelated crossover subplot.

The story should answer:

- Why does Haru become involved?
- How does the search for Subaru lead him toward the Metaverse?
- How does the Phantom Thieves storyline affect him?
- How do the other characters perceive Haru?
- How does Haru affect their story?
- What does Haru learn about himself?
- How does the Metaverse change his understanding of reality?
- How does his relationship with Subaru influence his actions?
- What does he sacrifice during this period?
- How does his Persona reflect his psychology?

The P5 Royal storyline should be treated as one of the major pillars of the Earth saga.

10. HARU'S PERSONA

Haru's initial Persona is:

DON QUIXOTE

Don Quixote represents Haru's early psychological state.

He refuses to accept that Subaru is gone.

Other people may tell him to move on.

Reality may repeatedly tell him that his search is impossible.

Haru continues anyway.

The symbolism includes:

- Obsession
- Idealism
- Defiance
- Refusal to accept an unbearable reality
- Chasing an apparently impossible goal
- Charging toward danger because stopping would mean accepting defeat

Do not constantly explain this symbolism in dialogue.

Let the story demonstrate it.

11. PERSONA EVOLUTION

Haru's Persona eventually evolves into:

THESEUS

This should happen because Haru has genuinely changed.

The evolution is not simply:

"New arc = stronger Persona."

It should represent the transformation of his identity.

Early Haru:

**"I need to find my brother."**

Later Haru:

**"I have found my brother. Now I need to protect him and help him survive."**

Theseus symbolizes the person who has entered the labyrinth and learned how to navigate it.

The metaphor becomes particularly important after Haru reaches Lugunica.

Haru is no longer merely chasing someone.

He is navigating an enormous maze of:

- Politics
- Magic
- Witch Cults
- Royal Selection conflict
- Hidden identities
- Supernatural threats
- Impossible situations
- Subaru's secrets

The evolution from Don Quixote to Theseus should therefore feel like a major psychological milestone.

12. THE EARTH ARC'S END

The **final major confrontation of the Earth Arc is the battle against Yaldabaoth**, following the Persona 5 Royal storyline.

This battle is not merely the climax of the Persona crossover.

It is the event that transitions Haru from Earth into the Re world.

The confrontation with Yaldabaoth should represent the culmination of everything Haru has experienced on Earth.

After the final battle, events lead directly to Haru being transported to the new world.

This is the actual end of the Earth saga.

The new world should therefore feel like:

**the beginning of an entirely new chapter in a life that has already become enormously complicated.**

Do not rush through the transition.

Give the Earth Arc a proper climax and emotional conclusion before beginning Lugunica.

13. THE FIGHTING STYLE

Haru's fighting style is extremely important.

His physical combat should resemble **professional wrestling from WWE/NJPW**, except that the combat is real.

Haru fights using techniques and physicality inspired by professional wrestling:

- Strikes
- Grapples
- Throws
- Slams
- Clinches
- Counters
- Suplex-style attacks
- Power moves
- Improvised environmental attacks
- Momentum-based attacks
- Brutal takedowns

But there is one essential difference:

**Haru is not pretending.**

He is not working with an opponent.

There are no choreographed protections.

There is no pulling punches because "that is how wrestling works."

When Haru performs a wrestling-style move, it is an actual fight.

A slam means the opponent is genuinely being slammed.

A strike means he is actually trying to hurt the opponent.

This creates a distinct visual identity for his combat.

It should feel like:

**WWE/NJPW movement vocabulary + genuine street violence.**

Do not turn every fight into an unnecessarily gruesome description.

The brutality should emerge naturally from the physicality.

14. HARU IS NOT AJIN

Important correction from previous versions of the concept:

**REMOVE THE AJIN ELEMENT ENTIRELY.**

Haru is not an Ajin.

He does not possess Ajin immortality.

He does not regenerate from lethal injuries.

He cannot casually sacrifice his body because he knows he will recover.

Do not reference Ajin physiology, IBM, Ajin immortality, or similar abilities.

Any supernatural abilities Haru eventually possesses must come from the actual story framework we establish.

15. CANON SHOULD CONTINUE TO RUN

The Re world should generally attempt to follow its canon trajectory.

However, Haru's presence naturally creates deviations.

Think of the story as:

**Canon trajectory + Haru's existence + Haru's decisions = altered history.**

Do NOT rewrite canon simply because an OC exists.

At the same time, do not force canon events to happen exactly as they did regardless of Haru's actions.

If Haru changes something, consequences may follow.

Sometimes those consequences will matter enormously.

Sometimes they will barely matter.

Sometimes Haru may make an apparently major decision and events may still converge toward a familiar outcome.

This uncertainty is intentional.

16. HARU DOES NOT HAVE PLOT FOREKNOWLEDGE

Haru is fully native to his world.

He does not know:

- Re
- Lugunica
- Subaru's future
- Witch Cult plans
- Royal Selection outcomes
- Return by Death
- Future deaths
- Future arcs
- Canon characters beyond what he actually learns
- Any meta information about the story

He reacts using the information available to him.

Never write Haru as though he is secretly the audience.

17. SUBARU AND HARU

The relationship between the brothers is the heart of the story.

Their dynamic should remain believable as a sibling relationship.

They should be capable of:

- Teasing each other
- Fighting verbally
- Annoying one another
- Protecting each other
- Misunderstanding one another
- Being genuinely affectionate
- Keeping secrets
- Getting angry
- Making jokes
- Showing vulnerability

Do not make every brotherly interaction emotional.

Do not make every interaction comedic either.

The relationship should feel lived-in.

The fact that they grew up together should be obvious in how they speak to each other.

18. SUBARU'S SECRET

When Haru eventually reunites with Subaru in Lugunica, Subaru is carrying the burden of **Return by Death**.

Haru should not immediately understand it.

Subaru must decide how much he can tell Haru.

This creates the central contrast:

Subaru's external persona

Confident.

Strategic.

Almost frighteningly perceptive.

Apparently capable of predicting future events.

Able to formulate plans that seem impossible.

Subaru's internal reality

Terrified.

Exhausted.

Traumatized.

Desperate.

Trying desperately to prevent people from dying.

Afraid that Haru will discover the truth.

Afraid that revealing the truth will somehow make everything worse.

Haru, meanwhile, knows his older brother extremely well.

That means Subaru's façade is going to be harder to maintain around Haru than around strangers.

Use this tension.

19. HARU'S ROLE IN LUGUNICA

Once Haru reaches Lugunica, Subaru increasingly becomes the strategist and Haru becomes the physical force capable of executing dangerous plans.

A useful shorthand is:

**Subaru = the mind.**
**Haru = the fist.**

But do not make this simplistic.

Haru is not stupid.

Subaru is not physically helpless.

Both brothers should contribute to solving problems.

Haru can recognize patterns, make judgments, question Subaru's plans, and act independently.

Sometimes Haru should make the correct decision when Subaru would have chosen incorrectly.

Sometimes Haru should make things worse.

Sometimes Subaru's strategy should fail because Haru changes it.

That interaction is where the story becomes interesting.

20. USE EXISTING SOURCE MATERIAL TO BUILD THE STORY

When creating a chapter, arc, character, or event:

FIRST search the available source material.

Ask:

**"Have we already written something that can serve this purpose?"**

If yes:

Adapt it.

If no:

Determine whether a combination of several smaller existing pieces can accomplish it.

Only after those options are exhausted should you create significant new material.

New connective writing is allowed.

Generic replacement writing is not.

21. CONTINUITY TRACKING

Maintain a clear understanding of:

- Haru's age
- Subaru's age
- Their relationship history
- Haru's Earth experiences
- Haru's Persona development
- Relationships formed on Earth
- Events from Persona 5 Royal
- Haru's psychological development
- His fighting development
- Information Haru possesses
- Information Subaru possesses
- Information the audience possesses
- What Haru believes about Subaru
- What Subaru is hiding
- Canon events
- Events altered by Haru
- Consequences of previous decisions

When necessary, explicitly maintain a timeline.

22. STORY DEVELOPMENT PROCESS

Do not immediately start writing chapters.

First analyze the available material.

Then produce:

Phase 1 — Source Audit

Identify useful existing material.

Phase 2 — Character Synthesis

Determine which existing character concepts can be adapted.

Phase 3 — Earth Arc Architecture

Construct the complete Earth storyline.

Phase 4 — Persona 5 Royal Integration

Determine how the P5 Royal storyline fits into Haru's search for Subaru.

Phase 5 — Earth Arc Character Development

Track Haru's psychological and physical development.

Phase 6 — Yaldabaoth Climax

Build the final Earth confrontation.

Phase 7 — Transition to Lugunica

Design how the end of the Yaldabaoth conflict leads to Haru's arrival in the Re world.

Phase 8 — Canon Divergence Mapping

Determine where Haru enters canon and what his presence changes.

Phase 9 — Lugunica Story Architecture

Build the Re portion around Haru and Subaru's reunion.

Phase 10 — Scene-Level Planning

Only after the larger architecture is established should individual scenes be developed in detail.

23. WHEN SOMETHING IS MISSING

If an important plot connection does not exist in the source material, do not immediately fill the gap with a giant AI-written sequence.

Instead:

1. Identify the missing connection.
2. Explain what the scene needs to accomplish.
3. Search the source material for compatible components.
4. Combine those components if possible.
5. Create only the necessary new connective structure.
6. Leave final prose for the human writer.

24. WHEN SOURCES CONFLICT

Do not quietly pretend contradictions do not exist.

Identify them.

Determine whether they can be reconciled through:

- Timeline changes
- Recontextualization
- Character changes
- Different circumstances
- Alternate outcomes
- Combining multiple versions
- Removing redundant material

If an important conflict cannot reasonably be resolved, present the problem rather than silently making an arbitrary decision.

25. THE FINAL STORY SHOULD FEEL LIKE A HUMAN PROJECT

The worst possible result is a story that technically follows the outline but reads like generic AI fanfiction.

Avoid that.

The final work should feel like someone took years of existing creative material, studied it closely, selected the strongest components, and painstakingly assembled them into a coherent long-form narrative.

The AI should provide the organizational intelligence needed to make that possible.

The human should provide the final voice.

26. THE MOST IMPORTANT RULES

Keep these rules active throughout the entire project:

**RULE 1 — SOURCE FIRST**

The attached repository is the primary creative resource.

**RULE 2 — DO NOT GENERATE GENERIC REPLACEMENTS**

Do not replace existing human-written material with generic AI prose.

**RULE 3 — REASSEMBLE**

Existing material can be reorganized, combined, adapted, and repurposed.

**RULE 4 — HUMAN PROSE**

The human author writes the final prose unless explicitly instructed otherwise.

**RULE 5 — EARTH IS A FULL ARC**

The Earth storyline is long and substantial.

**RULE 6 — PERSONA 5 ROYAL MATTERS**

Persona 5 Royal is a major component of the Earth Arc.

**RULE 7 — YALDABAOTH ENDS EARTH**

The Yaldabaoth confrontation is the culmination of the Earth saga and leads to Haru's transition into the new world.

**RULE 8 — HARU IS NATIVE**

He has no meta knowledge and no Re foreknowledge.

**RULE 9 — CANON HAS MOMENTUM**

The world tries to follow its established trajectory, but Haru can cause divergence.

**RULE 1 — FIGHTING STYLE**

Haru fights using WWE/NJPW-inspired wrestling techniques as genuine combat.

**RULE 12 — CHARACTER BEFORE SPECTACLE**

The story must remain grounded in character motivation and consequences.

Before writing any story:

**Inspect the entire attached source repository and the relevant previous project history.**

Do not produce a chapter.

Do not produce a generic outline based only on this prompt.

First determine what material we actually have.

Report:

- Major source works
- Useful scenes
- Useful characters
- Character dynamics
- Plot concepts
- Earth-based material
- Persona-related material
- Reusable action sequences
- Reusable emotional beats
- Potential Haru material
- Possible Subaru/Haru dynamics
- Material that could become the Earth Arc
- Material that could become the Persona storyline
- Material useful after the transition to Lugunica
- Contradictions
- Gaps
- Particularly strong pieces of writing that should be preserved or adapted
- Your assessment of how the existing material can be assembled into the Haru Natsuki Saga

Do not write the actual story yet.

**Study the material first.**

The story we eventually create should be a story that could only have emerged from the material I provided — not something an AI would have independently invented from the phrase "Re OC fanfiction."

The objective is not to demonstrate how much you can write.

The objective is to demonstrate how well you can understand, organize, and transform the material I already gave you.
