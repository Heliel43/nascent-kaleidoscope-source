## 2. Human records and TVA mechanics

### 2.1 What the ordinary records say

At opening, Henry's accessible records contain:

- the name Henry Cooper;
- a foundling/child-services placement in Queens in 2000;
- an estimated birth date in early 2000;
- no reliable hospital birth certificate;
- a mother field that is blank, sealed, or marked unknown;
- no father field;
- Elena Reyes as long-term guardian;
- normal school, vaccination, dental, and medical history from 2000 onward.

These records are not flawless. The system's error is initially plausible because foundling records are often incomplete. The strange part is the pattern: old documents do not merely have typos; they disagree about what kind of record Henry is supposed to have.

### 2.2 What happened in 1972

The TVA flags an infant anomaly in a branch where Loki's human relationship has produced a child with nonhuman biological markers. The TVA classification is bureaucratic and utilitarian: a branch-level problem, a non-native biological signature, and a chronology that does not fit the local record.

The TVA captures Henry as an infant rather than immediately pruning the branch because the operation is also an attempt to contain and examine the anomaly. This is not because Henry is destined to save the multiverse.

During the operation, Sylvie is escaping the TVA. From her point of view, the escape and infant theft are simultaneous. She takes Henry because the TVA's treatment of him resembles the destruction of her own childhood.

### 2.3 Why the 2000 drop is possible

The TVA exists outside ordinary Earth chronology. Sylvie uses a damaged/stolen TemPad during the escape and selects a low-visibility insertion point on the Sacred Timeline in Queens in 2000. She cannot safely return Henry to the 1972 event without exposing him to the same TVA operation, so she chooses a point where a foundling infant can plausibly enter human systems.

She does not age him. He is an infant when dropped and grows normally.

### 2.4 How the concealment enchantment works

The enchantment:

- masks Jotun/Asgardian biological signatures from ordinary tests;
- dampens the temporal irregularity that would make TVA detection immediate;
- makes his power look like an unusual human/metahuman ability;
- does not create a detailed false family history;
- does not rewrite adult memories;
- does not make Henry immune to later magic or timeline pressure;
- weakens at the edges under Asgardian artifacts, TVA technology, major magical events, and Henry's increasing power control.

The enchantment is concealment, not a complete personality or memory spell.

### 2.5 Why the TVA does not retrieve him immediately

The TVA case file records the anomaly as lost during the escape. The 2000 foundling's temporal signature is quiet enough to read as an ordinary human case. The concealment enchantment and the damaged transfer create enough noise that the TVA does not connect Henry's human records to the original infant file until later timeline instability makes the match visible.

This explanation is not available to early characters. They first experience only inconsistent records.

## 3. Mother/1971 evidence chain

### 3.1 Eleanor's life and evidence

Eleanor Cooper is a 22-year-old woman in the Portland/Seattle corridor in 1971. She works an ordinary clerical/telephone job connected loosely to the airline/hotel world and meets Loki, using the name Dan Cooper, during the short period surrounding the hijacking.

The relationship lasts days, not months. Loki is charming, evasive, and not planning a child. Eleanor is curious, practical, and not written as someone who instantly sees a god beneath a disguise.

Eleanor's surviving materials are:

1. a notebook with dates, weather, conversation fragments, and her uncertainty;
2. a photograph with partial identifying details rather than a convenient full portrait;
3. a small metal token of unknown composition, left or lost by Loki;
4. a later private medical/birth record that does not enter Henry's 2000 foster file;
5. an estate/archive trail after her 2008 death.

### 3.2 Staged evidence schedule

| Phase | Evidence | What Henry/Peter think | What the audience may infer |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | approximate 2000 foundling birth date | paperwork is incomplete | Henry's age is not fully documented |
| A2 | name/object from foster intake | sentimental or ordinary | Cooper may be inherited from an older source |
| A3 | conflicting dates and sealed file | bureaucratic error | there is a second story behind the record |
| A4 pre-confirmation | metal token and notebook reference to Dan Cooper | possible historical connection | Dan is linked to D.B. Cooper, but not yet Loki |
| A4 confirmation | biological match to Loki's Battle of New York sample | impossible paternity | D.B. Cooper was Loki; Henry is his son |
| A5–A9 | selective archive and Thor/Loki conversations | family history is incomplete | the age mystery survives the paternity answer |
| A11–A14 | post-Snap/time-heist inconsistencies | the record is becoming impossible | the problem is temporal, not merely clerical |
| A15 | TVA infant file | direct historical truth | 1972 capture and 2000 drop |
| A15 late | Sylvie's account | why Henry survived | the concealment was an act of desperate care and control |

### 3.3 AOU confirmation mechanics

The AOU evidence requires all of the following:

- Henry is in a controlled enough Avengers-adjacent setting for a medical/technical check;
- an unusual power reaction or injury makes testing reasonable;
- Bruce/Tony/another technical team finds that Henry's markers do not read as ordinary human;
- a sealed Battle of New York sample from Loki is available;
- comparison reveals a biological match rather than generic alien similarity;
- Eleanor's papers identify the 1971 human connection;
- Thor recognizes the private D.B. Cooper story.

No single database, Mind Stone, or spell solves the parentage.

### K1 — after first Peter partnership

Peter knows Henry's power, mask, basic home pressure, and that the records are incomplete. He does not know the father or TVA truth.

### K2 — after A3

A small adult circle knows the boys exist and may have a file. Natasha/Tony/Bruce know more behavior than Henry realizes. No one has solved the origin.

### K3 — AOU reveal

Henry, Peter, Elena, Thor, selected Avengers, and Loki know or strongly accept the paternity truth. They do not know why Henry's actual conception/birth dates do not match his apparent age. Sylvie and the TVA remain hidden.

### K4 — post-AOU

Thor and Loki know that Henry's records are still impossible. Strange later knows concealment magic is involved but not who cast it or why.

### K5 — post-Endgame/multiverse

The existence of timeline branches and variants is known. Henry's personal origin is still not confirmed. The 2012 Loki variant is not the father.

### K6 — TVA reveal

Henry learns the 1972 capture, Sylvie's rescue, 2000 drop, and concealment. He still has to decide what those facts mean emotionally.

## 6. Thor/Henry contact ledger

### Pre-reveal encounters — maximum useful set

1. **A3, professional sighting:** Thor sees Henry during a contained rescue involving unusual equipment or Asgardian aftermath. He gives one tactical correction and leaves.
2. **A4, brief battlefield exchange:** Henry creates a route/barrier during AOU preparation or response; Thor recognizes his control, not his blood.
3. **A4, short ordinary conversation:** Thor remembers Henry's name and asks a practical question. Henry is not invited into family life.

These encounters must not contain:

- private emotional confession;
- Thor calling Henry family;
- protective attachment;
- mentorship promises;
- Thor silently knowing the truth;
- Henry feeling that Thor is already the father-shaped adult in his life.

### Post-reveal progression

- shock;
- concern;
- an overprotective mistake;
- boundary conversation;
- practical help;
- reliable presence;
- earned family language, if Henry chooses it.

## 8. Power-state ledger

### P0 — Awakening

**Available:** accidental frost, cooling a touched object, small moisture freeze, thin ice film.
**Range:** touch/very close.
**Cost:** surprise, numb fingers, thirst, headaches after repeated attempts.
**Cannot:** make a useful weapon reliably, freeze a person, create ice in dry air at scale.

### P1 — First masked period

**Available:** wedges, hand-sized shield, cuffs on objects/limbs if contact is controlled, slippery patch, short brace, crude ramp.
**Range:** a few metres with line of sight; precision poor.
**Cost:** hydration/calories, concentration, broken constructs, poor balance.
**Cannot:** safely restrain a living body by freezing deep tissue, make a wall tall enough to stop a vehicle, fight while exhausted.

### P2 — Peter partnership

**Available:** ice slide, short platform, route control, pipe cooling, layered buckler, better non-lethal restraint, emergency wall.
**Range:** modest line-of-sight extension.
**Cost:** several uses cause numbness/migraine; hot/dry interiors remain difficult.
**Breakthrough:** movement and placement, not bigger attacks.

### P3 — AOU

**Available:** drone-joint freezing, evacuation barriers, short bridges, multiple small constructs, brief partial ice-body adaptation.
**Range:** battlefield-relevant but local.
**Cost:** severe exhaustion and recovery; blunt force can shatter him; heat and energy attacks bypass defenses.
**Cannot:** solo Ultron, freeze a city, stop Sokovia's destruction, or become invulnerable.

### P4 — post-AOU/Civil War/Homecoming

**Available:** more efficient thermal sinks, condensation, safer ice skin, movement in heat, team barriers.
**Cost:** still environmental and metabolic.
**Story purpose:** judgment, consent, and non-use become progress.

### P5 — Ragnarok/Infinity War

**Available:** large rescue structures with preparation, cold-storage stabilization, coordinated terrain control, limited atmospheric work.
**Cost:** dangerous dehydration, body-temperature crash, delayed recovery.
**Cannot:** overpower Hela, Thanos, or Infinity Stone effects.

### P6 — post-Blip

**Available:** refined sensory control and better efficiency after training.
**Cost:** memory/temporal stress can disrupt concealment and control.
**Story purpose:** power is stable enough that identity, not basic survival, becomes the main problem.

### P7 — multiverse/TVA

**Available:** thermal manipulation remains physical; it can stabilize an object or route but cannot control time.
**Cost:** TVA technology and reality failure ignore ordinary ice rules.
**Hard rule:** Henry is not the source of timeline stability.

## 10. Injury and recovery ledger

No injury exists merely to make Henry suffer. Every injury changes a technique, relationship, or rule.

| Phase | Injury/consequence | Carry-forward effect |
|---|---|---|
| A1 | minor numbness, bruised shoulder, dehydration | Elena/ Peter rules about stopping |
| A2 | construct shatters while he is using it; wrist/hand strain | better bracing and gloves |
| A3 | meaningful thermal exhaustion or concussion-like migraine | adult medical check and honesty conflict |
| A4 | cold-body layer shatters; severe hypothermia risk | proves he is not invulnerable; power pacing changes |
| A5–A7 | ordinary injuries from school/hero overlap | home and partnership logistics |
| A8–A9 | magical/energy overload bypasses ice | learns some threats cannot be countered by element |
| A10 | battlefield exhaustion, then Snap | no heroic last stand beyond physical limits |
| A11 | return disorientation and appetite/sleep disruption | post-Blip care |
| A13–A15 | memory/temporal symptoms rather than gratuitous bodily damage | investigation and identity pressure |

## 11. Canon event invariants

These must remain true unless a later approved divergence explicitly records why:

- Loki's 1971 D.B. Cooper stunt is real and remains a strange joke before it becomes evidence.
- Thor and Loki's adopted-brother relationship remains central.
- Henry is not present as a child combatant in the 2012 New York invasion.
- *The Dark World* is not the paternity reveal.
- HYDRA's S.H.I.E.L.D. infiltration and the Winter Soldier plot remain intact.
- Peter is absent from the AOU Avengers field team.
- Ultron awakens through Tony/Bruce's choices and the scepter/Mind Stone context.
- Wanda/Pietro, Vision, Sokovia, and the Avengers' AOU victory remain recognizable.
- Peter is recruited by Tony in *Civil War*.
- Henry does not replace Vulture as Peter's central *Homecoming* problem.
- Thor, Loki, Hela, Valkyrie, Hulk, and Surtur retain their *Ragnarok* functions.
- Loki dies in *Infinity War* and the 2012 variant is separate.
- Peter is Snapped and returns in *Endgame*; Henry is also Snapped for cohort continuity.
- Wanda's Westview story is not Henry's origin story.
- Peter's *No Way Home* memory spell affects Henry; he is not conveniently immune.
- The TVA becomes explicit only after the story has earned multiverse access.
