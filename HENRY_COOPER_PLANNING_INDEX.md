## Locked high-level decisions

- The story starts in fall 2014.
- Henry's powers are thermal/cryokinetic manipulation with environmental limits and real physical costs.
- Peter's unseen early patrol period is the smallest chronology butterfly allowing a same-class friendship before *Civil War*.
- Peter teaches Henry superheroing after intervening in a dangerous novice mistake.
- Henry discovers Peter's identity first because Peter is terrible at hiding it.
- Thor may meet Henry before the reveal, but there is no pre-reveal closeness, protectiveness, emotional attachment, or bond.
- The real Thor/Henry relationship begins after the Loki parentage reveal.
- Loki is the real D.B. Cooper, briefly involved with Eleanor Cooper in 1971, and did not know she became pregnant.
- Henry is born in 1972, captured as an infant by the TVA, taken by Sylvie during her simultaneous escape, dropped as an infant onto the Sacred Timeline in Queens in 2000, and concealed by enchantment.
- Henry grows normally after the 2000 drop; there is no unexplained magical aging.
- The parentage reveal occurs in the *Age of Ultron* era through a combined evidence chain, not in *The Dark World* and not through the Mind Stone as an oracle.
- Ultron, Sokovia, the Avengers, Peter's canon recruitment, and the later MCU events remain recognizable.
- Henry is Snapped with Peter, preserving their cohort after *Endgame*.
- The TVA/multiverse explanation arrives only after the MCU timeline has earned it.
- Chapter prose is the last step; the next work is the detailed Arc 1 chapter packet.
